Security Analyst
Greenfield State Information Office — On site, Greenfield, OH

The Greenfield State Information Office is seeking a Security Analyst to help protect the systems and data that support public services. Working on site in Greenfield, the analyst monitors agency networks, reviews security alerts, assesses risks and works with technical staff and program offices to keep information systems secure and in line with agency policy.

Responsibilities of the position
- Monitor security logs from agency systems and networks to detect suspicious activity.
- Triage security alerts, separate real threats from false positives and escalate confirmed incidents.
- Write and maintain security policy documentation, standards and procedures.
- Record findings and actions taken so that incidents and reviews can be audited.
- Work with system owners to confirm that identified issues are resolved.

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 4 or more years of experience in information security analysis.
- Working knowledge of vulnerability scanning and penetration testing.
- Experience with Splunk for log analysis and alert review.
- Ability to script in PowerShell and Python.
- Hands-on experience in incident response.
- Security Analyst Certificate is required; Incident Handler Certificate is preferred.
