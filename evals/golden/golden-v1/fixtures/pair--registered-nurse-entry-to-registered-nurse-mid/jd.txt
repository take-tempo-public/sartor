Registered Nurse
Summit Health Partners — On site, Charlotte, NC

Position summary
Summit Health Partners, a multi-state hospital company, seeks a Registered Nurse to provide direct patient care on site in Charlotte, NC. The Registered Nurse assesses patients, carries out plans of care, and works with physicians and interdisciplinary teams to deliver safe and consistent care.
Minimum:
- Active Registered Nurse Licence
- Experience in direct patient care
Preferred:
- Experience with Epic Systems

Responsibilities
- Teach patients and families about diagnoses, medications and discharge instructions
- Chart assessments, interventions and outcomes accurately in the electronic health record
- Administer medications safely and monitor patients for effects and reactions
- Assess patients and report changes in condition to physicians and the care team
- Take part in bedside handoff to support continuity of care

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 4 or more years of experience as a Registered Nurse
- Required: active Registered Nurse Licence
- Working knowledge of Epic Systems and clinical documentation standards
- Skill in triage, patient assessment and care planning
- Experience with telemetry monitoring
- Preferred: Nurse Manager Certificate
