Registered Nurse
Summit Health Partners — On site, Charlotte, NC

Position summary
Summit Health Partners, a multi-state hospital company, seeks a Registered Nurse to deliver safe, evidence-based patient care on site in Charlotte, NC. The Registered Nurse assesses patients, administers medications, educates patients and families, and maintains accurate records in collaboration with the interdisciplinary care team. Minimum qualifications include an active registered nurse licence, current resuscitation certification and relevant clinical experience. Preferred qualifications include leadership training.

Responsibilities
- Teach patients and families about diagnoses, medications, self-care and discharge instructions, confirming understanding before each patient leaves.
- Chart assessments, interventions and outcomes accurately and promptly in the electronic health record.
- Administer medications safely according to provider orders, policy and the rights of medication administration.
- Monitor patient responses to treatment and report significant changes to the provider and charge nurse.
- Communicate patient status clearly at shift change to support continuity of care.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Minimum: 4 or more years of experience as a registered nurse.
- Minimum: working proficiency in Epic Systems and clinical documentation standards.
- Minimum: demonstrated skill in triage, patient assessment, care planning and telemetry monitoring.
- Minimum: active registered nurse licence and a current Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card.
- Preferred: Nurse Manager Certificate.
