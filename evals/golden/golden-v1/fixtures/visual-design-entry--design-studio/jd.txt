Junior Graphic Designer
Willow and Ash Studio — On site, Portland, OR

About us and the job
Willow and Ash Studio is a small, owner-run design studio in Portland. We make brand identities, packaging and print work for our clients, and we do that work together in our studio. We are looking for a junior graphic designer to join us on site, help with day-to-day design tasks and learn how a small studio takes a project from first brief to final file.

What the job involves
- Prepare print-ready files for printers, checking bleed, color and fonts before they go out
- Update brand guidelines when a client's identity changes or grows
- Make client revisions carefully and keep track of each version
- Build layouts for print and digital pieces from approved concepts
- Keep project files organized so everyone in the studio can find what they need

What you need
- 2 or more years of experience in graphic design
- A good grasp of typography and color theory
- Experience with photo retouching in Adobe Photoshop
- Solid layout design skills for print and digital work
- Comfort making quick graphics in Canva
- Willingness to work on site in our Portland studio
