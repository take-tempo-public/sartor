Electrical Foreman
Northplain Municipal Utilities — On site, Northplain, IA

Duties
Under general direction of the electrical superintendent, the Electrical Foreman supervises a crew that installs, maintains and repairs electrical systems in utility-owned buildings, pump stations and public facilities. The position assigns daily work, inspects completed installations, keeps job records and serves as the department contact for electrical work on assigned projects. This is a working supervisory position based on site in Northplain.

Responsibilities of the position
- Direct helpers and apprentices on daily assignments and check the quality of their work.
- Read plans, drawings and specifications to lay out electrical work before it begins.
- Plan jobs, set work sequences and coordinate with other utility crews.
- Prepare material lists and order materials, tools and equipment for each job.
- Inspect completed installations and keep records of work performed.

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 14 or more years of experience in electrical construction or maintenance.
- Wire pulling, troubleshooting and lighting installation on field projects.
- Code compliance knowledge sufficient to prepare work for inspection.
- Load calculations and blueprint reading for planning and layout.
- A current State Apprentice Registration Card is required at the time of appointment.
