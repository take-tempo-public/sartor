Electrical Foreman
Northplain Municipal Utilities — On site, Northplain, IA

Duties: Northplain Municipal Utilities is seeking an Electrical Foreman to lead electrical crews on site in Northplain, IA. The Electrical Foreman supervises installation, maintenance and repair of electrical systems at utility facilities, keeps work safe and up to code, and reports job progress to utility management. This is a working supervisory position that requires both field skill and crew leadership.

Responsibilities of the position
- Direct helpers and apprentices on daily assignments and check the quality of their work
- Read plans and drawings to lay out electrical work at utility facilities
- Plan jobs, set crew schedules and sequence tasks to limit service disruption
- Order materials and track supplies so crews have what they need on site
- Enforce safe work practices and inspect completed work before sign-off

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 14 or more years of experience in electrical work
- Current Master Electrician Licence required
- Wire pulling, troubleshooting and lighting installation skills
- Code compliance knowledge applied to installation and repair work
- Load calculations and blueprint reading for planning and layout
