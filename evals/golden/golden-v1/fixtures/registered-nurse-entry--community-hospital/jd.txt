Graduate Registered Nurse
Pinebrook Medical Center — On site, Toledo, OH

Summary
Pinebrook Medical Center is a community hospital committed to safe, compassionate care for every patient and family we serve. We are seeking a Graduate Registered Nurse to join our on-site nursing team in Toledo, OH. Candidates must hold a current Registered Nurse Licence and a Basic Life Support Card.

Duties
- Assess patients on admission and throughout each shift, and report changes in condition promptly
- Coordinate with physicians and the wider care team to carry out and update plans of care
- Give medications safely and accurately according to orders and hospital policy
- Monitor patient responses to treatment and record findings in the medical record
- Teach patients and families about their condition, medications and care after discharge
- Take part in bedside handoff to keep care consistent between shifts

Licensure and qualifications
- Basic Life Support Card and an active Registered Nurse Licence are required; Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card is preferred
- 2 or more years of registered nursing experience
- Patient assessment and vital signs monitoring skills across varied patient needs
- Care planning and triage in a busy hospital setting
- Clinical documentation that is accurate, timely and complete
- Infection control practices in line with hospital policy
