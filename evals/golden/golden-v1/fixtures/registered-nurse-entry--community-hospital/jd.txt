Graduate Registered Nurse
Pinebrook Medical Center — On site, Toledo, OH

Summary
Pinebrook Medical Center is a community hospital in Toledo, OH, committed to safe, compassionate care for every neighbor who walks through our doors. We are seeking a Graduate Registered Nurse to join our on-site nursing team and grow under the guidance of experienced staff. Candidates must hold a current Registered Nurse Licence and a Basic Life Support Card.

Duties
- Assess patients on admission and throughout each shift, and report changes in condition to the charge nurse.
- Coordinate with physicians and the wider care team on treatment plans and new orders.
- Prepare and give medications safely, following hospital policy and physician orders.
- Monitor vital signs and respond promptly to abnormal findings.
- Record assessments, interventions and patient responses accurately in the health record.
- Teach patients and families about their care, medications and discharge instructions.

Licensure and qualifications
- Current Registered Nurse Licence (required).
- Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card (preferred).
- 2 or more years of nursing experience.
- Working skill in patient assessment, vital signs monitoring, care planning and triage.
- Sound clinical documentation habits and knowledge of infection control practices.
