Graduate Registered Nurse
Pinebrook Medical Center — On site, Toledo, OH

Summary
Pinebrook Medical Center is a community hospital in Toledo, OH. Our mission is to provide safe, compassionate care close to home for every patient who comes through our doors. We are seeking a Graduate Registered Nurse to join our on-site nursing team and grow with experienced colleagues. A current registered nurse licence and a Basic Life Support Card are required for this position.

Duties
- Assess patients on admission and throughout each shift, recording findings in the patient record
- Coordinate with physicians and the wider care team on treatment plans and changes in patient condition
- Give medications as ordered, checking each dose, route and time before administration
- Monitor vital signs and escalate concerns promptly to the charge nurse or physician
- Take part in bedside handoff so incoming staff have a clear picture of each patient
- Teach patients and families about their care, medications and discharge instructions

Licensure and qualifications
- 2 or more years of experience as a registered nurse
- Patient assessment and vital signs monitoring skills in a hospital or clinic setting
- Working knowledge of care planning and triage
- Accurate clinical documentation and consistent infection control practice
- Current registered nurse licence and Basic Life Support Card required; Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card preferred
