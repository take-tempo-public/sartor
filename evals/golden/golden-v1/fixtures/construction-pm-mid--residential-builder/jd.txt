Project Manager
Elmcrest Builders — On site, Boise, ID

Elmcrest Builders is an owner-run residential builder in Boise. We are looking for a Project Manager to run our home builds on site from start to finish. You will work closely with our crews, our trade partners and the homeowners we build for, and you will report directly to the owners.

What the job involves
- Schedule permits and inspections so each stage of the build stays on track
- Run regular meetings with homeowners to review progress, decisions and changes
- Coordinate subcontractors and suppliers on site so trades arrive in the right order
- Keep the schedule and budget for each home up to date
- Walk the site often and deal with problems before they slow the work

What you need
- 4 or more years of experience managing construction projects
- Working knowledge of Oracle Primavera, Procore and Bluebeam Revu
- Safety planning experience on active job sites
- Contract administration skills, including handling scope changes with owners and trades
- Punch list closeout experience through final walkthrough and handover
- Required certification: Construction Safety Manager Certificate
