Senior HVAC Technician
Corliss Commercial Mechanical — On site, Columbus, OH

Position summary
Corliss Commercial Mechanical seeks a Senior HVAC Technician to install, service and repair heating, cooling and controls equipment at customer sites in Columbus, OH. The role estimates work, leads preventive maintenance and guides apprentices. Minimum requirements are an HVAC technician licence and refrigerant compliance certification. Experience with building automation is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Estimate jobs for customers, including labour, materials and equipment options
- Plan and carry out preventive maintenance visits on commercial heating and cooling systems
- Diagnose and repair faults in heating, cooling and controls equipment
- Train apprentices in safe work practices, installation methods and service procedures
- Review completed work for quality and compliance before sign-off
- Keep accurate work orders and service records for every visit

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 8 or more years of experience as an HVAC technician
- Refrigerant handling and leak testing in line with compliance rules
- Furnace repair and thermostat installation on commercial systems
- Electrical testing of motors, controls and wiring
- Building automation setup and troubleshooting
- Controls Technician Certificate required
