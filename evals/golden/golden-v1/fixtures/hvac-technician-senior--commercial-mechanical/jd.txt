Senior HVAC Technician
Corliss Commercial Mechanical — On site, Columbus, OH

Corliss Commercial Mechanical seeks a Senior HVAC Technician to service, repair and maintain heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems in commercial buildings in the Columbus, OH area. The position is on site, leads technicians on assigned jobs and serves as a point of contact for customers. Minimum requirements include extensive field experience and a refrigerant compliance credential. Preferred candidates also hold a state HVAC licence and controls training.

Responsibilities
- Prepare accurate job estimates for customers, covering labour, materials and equipment.
- Plan and perform preventive maintenance visits on commercial heating and cooling systems.
- Diagnose and repair faults in furnaces, heat pumps, air handling equipment and controls.
- Train and supervise apprentices on safe work practices and service procedures.
- Review completed work and keep work orders and service records up to date.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 8 or more years of experience as an HVAC technician.
- Refrigerant handling and leak testing skills, with a current Refrigerant Compliance Certificate (required).
- Furnace repair, thermostat installation and electrical testing on commercial equipment.
- Building automation knowledge, including troubleshooting controls and sensors.
- Preferred: a state HVAC technician licence and formal controls training.
