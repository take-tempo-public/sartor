Hardware Engineering Manager
Fernhill Robotics — Hybrid, Pittsburgh, PA

Fernhill Robotics builds robots, and we need someone to run the hardware side. As our Hardware Engineering Manager, you'll lead a team of engineers, own the path from schematic to working board, and keep design quality high as we grow. You'll split your time between hands-on debugging and helping people grow as engineers. This is a hybrid role based in Pittsburgh.

What you'll do
- Lead design reviews for schematics, layouts and firmware, and make sure decisions are written down
- Mentor junior engineers through debugging, bring-up and review feedback
- Set review standards so design reviews catch problems early
- Plan hardware work with the robotics and software teams
- Own prototype testing and sign off boards before they go to build

What we're looking for
- 11 or more years of hardware or embedded engineering experience, including time leading engineers
- Hands-on experience with RTOS-based firmware and Linux systems
- Firmware bring-up on new boards, from first power-on to stable builds
- Oscilloscope debugging and lab work on mixed-signal hardware
- SPICE simulation for circuit design and checks
- Signal integrity analysis for high-speed boards
