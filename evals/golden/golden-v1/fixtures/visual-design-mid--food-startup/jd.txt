Graphic Designer
Kindle Street Foods — Hybrid, Brooklyn, NY

Kindle Street Foods is a small food-products startup, and our brand needs a designer who can own how it looks. You'll shape our products on shelves, in feeds and in print. Expect a hybrid setup in Brooklyn, a fast pace, and a team that wants your opinion.

What you'll do
- Build packaging mockups for new and existing products
- Design social media graphics for launches and everyday posts
- Prepare print-ready files for printers and check proofs before they run
- Keep our visual style consistent across packaging, social and print
- Work with the founders and marketing team on campaign ideas

What we're looking for
- 3 or more years of experience as a graphic designer
- Strong skills in Adobe Photoshop, including photo retouching for product shots
- Comfort with Canva for quick social content
- Hands-on print production experience, from file setup to press checks
- Working knowledge of motion graphics for short social clips
- A solid grasp of color theory and how it works on packaging
