Systems Engineer
Lattice Cloud — Remote (US)

Lattice Cloud runs hosting for teams who would rather ship than babysit servers. We're a small crew, and as a Systems Engineer you'll keep our platform steady, help shape how we handle incidents and changes, and have a real say in the tooling we pick. Expect plenty of ownership and not much ceremony.

What you'll do
- Take your turn on the on-call rotation and respond when alerts fire
- Write clear post-incident write-ups that explain what happened and what we'll change
- Review change requests and give change approvals before work reaches production
- Tune monitoring so alerts point to real problems instead of noise
- Work with the rest of the team to automate routine operations work

What we're looking for
- 4 or more years of experience in systems engineering or IT operations
- Hands-on work with Terraform and Python for infrastructure and automation
- Experience with Prometheus for monitoring and Splunk for log search
- Comfort with PowerShell scripting and with ServiceNow for tickets and change records
- Cloud Operations Certificate (required)
