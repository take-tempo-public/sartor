Junior Systems Administrator
Brennan IT Services — Hybrid, Atlanta, GA

Position summary
Brennan IT Services, a managed services provider, seeks a Junior Systems Administrator to support client infrastructure in a hybrid arrangement based in Atlanta, GA. The role maintains servers, monitoring and patching under the guidance of senior engineers. At minimum, candidates need scripting experience, familiarity with cloud platforms and a recognised systems administration certification. Preferred candidates also hold a cloud operations certification.

Responsibilities
- Support capacity planning by tracking resource usage across client environments and reporting trends to senior engineers.
- Tune monitoring alerts to reduce noise and make sure actionable issues reach the right team.
- Run scheduled patching cycles for client servers and confirm systems return to service afterward.
- Document changes and follow the change approval process for all production work.
- Respond to service tickets and escalate complex incidents to senior staff.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Required: 2 or more years of experience in systems administration or IT operations.
- Required: working knowledge of Python and Bash scripting for routine automation.
- Required: experience with Prometheus monitoring, Microsoft Azure, Terraform and Kubernetes.
- Required: Systems Administrator Certificate or an equivalent licence or certification.
- Preferred: Cloud Operations Certificate.
