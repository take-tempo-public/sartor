Senior Product Manager
Alderbrook Health Services — Hybrid, Minneapolis, MN

Summary
Alderbrook Health Services is a regional health system with a mission to deliver accessible, coordinated care to every community we serve. We are seeking a Senior Product Manager to guide the digital products that support patients, families and care teams. This hybrid role is based in Minneapolis, MN. Applicants must meet the licensure and qualification requirements listed in this posting.

Duties
- Lead vendor evaluations and pricing decisions for patient-facing and internal products.
- Build and maintain the product roadmap in partnership with clinical and operational teams.
- Set product strategy with executive leadership and align it with organizational goals.
- Translate patient and staff needs into clear requirements for engineering and design.
- Report on product performance and adjust plans based on results.

Licensure and qualifications
- 9 or more years of experience in product management.
- Experience running user interviews with patients, families or staff.
- Working knowledge of Tableau for reporting and dashboards.
- Pricing analysis skills applied to vendor and product decisions.
- Go-to-market planning experience for new products or features.
- Familiarity with Figma and Jira.
