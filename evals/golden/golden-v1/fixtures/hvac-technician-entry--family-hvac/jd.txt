HVAC Apprentice
Sutter Heating and Air — On site, Boise, ID

About us and the job
Sutter Heating and Air is a family HVAC contractor in Boise, run by its owners. We fix and look after heating and cooling systems for our neighbours, and we treat every home like our own. As an HVAC apprentice you will work on site with our technicians, learning the trade on real jobs and helping customers get their systems running again.

What the job involves
- Diagnose heating and cooling faults alongside an experienced technician
- Test systems for refrigerant leaks and help carry out repairs
- Prepare clear job estimates for customers before work begins
- Explain findings and costs to customers in plain words
- Keep work orders complete and up to date for each visit

What you need
- 2 or more years of experience in heating and cooling service
- Hands-on skill in preventive maintenance, leak testing and system diagnostics
- Experience with duct installation and thermostat installation
- Working knowledge of load calculations
- Refrigerant Compliance Certificate (required)
- HVAC Technician Licence (preferred)
