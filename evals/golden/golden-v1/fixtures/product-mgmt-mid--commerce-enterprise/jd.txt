Product Manager
Harbor and Vine Commerce — Hybrid, Seattle, WA

Position summary
Harbor and Vine Commerce seeks a Product Manager to own a defined area of its e-commerce platform from discovery through launch. The Product Manager will work with engineering, design, merchandising and operations to set priorities and deliver measurable outcomes for customers and vendors. Minimum qualifications include product management experience, clear requirements writing and sound judgment on pricing. Preferred qualifications include familiarity with product analytics tools.

Responsibilities
- Lead vendor evaluations and recommend pricing decisions supported by market and customer data.
- Plan and conduct customer interviews to identify needs and validate proposed solutions.
- Coordinate across engineering, design, marketing and operations teams to align on scope and timelines.
- Write clear requirements and maintain a prioritized product backlog.
- Track product performance after launch and adjust plans based on results.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 5 or more years of experience in product management or a related role.
- Working knowledge of prioritization frameworks for ranking features and investments.
- Experience with stakeholder alignment across technical and business teams.
- Skill in pricing analysis and in conducting user interviews.
- Preferred: experience with go-to-market planning and prototyping in Figma.
