Accounting Manager
Evergreen Community Foundation — Hybrid, Seattle, WA

Summary
Evergreen Community Foundation connects donors with the causes that strengthen our region. We are seeking an Accounting Manager to lead day-to-day accounting, keep accurate records, and support our grantmaking and audit work in a hybrid role based in Seattle. A Certified Public Accountant Licence is required for this position.

Duties
- Lead preparation for the annual financial audit and serve as the main contact for external auditors
- Complete monthly bank reconciliations and resolve discrepancies promptly
- Review vendor invoices for accuracy, coding and approval before payment
- Oversee the month-end close and prepare financial statements for leadership and the board
- Supervise accounting staff and maintain internal controls

Licensure and qualifications
- Certified Public Accountant Licence required
- 14 or more years of accounting experience
- Experience in audit support, accounts payable and journal entries
- Working knowledge of sales tax filing and account reconciliation
- Advanced skills in Microsoft Excel
