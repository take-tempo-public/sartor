Accounting Manager
Evergreen Community Foundation — Hybrid, Seattle, WA

Summary
Evergreen Community Foundation connects donors with the causes that strengthen local neighborhoods. We are seeking an Accounting Manager to oversee the foundation's accounting operations, guide the monthly close and keep our financial records ready for audit. This hybrid role is based in Seattle, WA. A Bookkeeping Certificate is required.

Duties
- Lead audit preparation, including supporting schedules and responses to auditor requests.
- Complete bank reconciliations each month and resolve any differences promptly.
- Review vendor invoices against approvals and contracts before payment.
- Oversee the monthly close and the accuracy of the general ledger.
- Supervise accounting staff and review their work for accuracy and completeness.

Licensure and qualifications
- 14 or more years of accounting experience.
- Bookkeeping Certificate required.
- Experience with audit support and journal entries.
- Working knowledge of accounts payable and sales tax filing.
- Account reconciliation skills and proficiency in Microsoft Excel.
