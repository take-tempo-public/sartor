Accounting Manager
Evergreen Community Foundation — Hybrid, Seattle, WA

Summary
Evergreen Community Foundation connects local donors with the causes that strengthen our region. The Accounting Manager leads day-to-day accounting for the foundation, oversees the close, prepares for the annual audit and keeps grant and donor funds accurately recorded. This hybrid role is based in Seattle, WA. A Certified Public Accountant Licence is required.

Duties
- Lead audit preparation, including schedules, supporting documents and responses to auditor requests.
- Complete bank reconciliations each month and resolve differences promptly.
- Review vendor invoices against approvals and contracts before payment.
- Oversee the month-end close and review entries prepared by staff.
- Prepare financial reports for leadership and the finance committee.
- Maintain internal controls over cash, payables and receivables.

Licensure and qualifications
- Certified Public Accountant Licence required.
- 14 or more years of accounting experience.
- Experience with audit support, accounts payable and sales tax filing.
- Strong command of journal entries and account reconciliation.
- Advanced working knowledge of Microsoft Excel.
