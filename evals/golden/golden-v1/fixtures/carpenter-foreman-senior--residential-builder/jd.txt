Master Carpenter
Fieldstone Builders — On site, Lancaster, PA

Fieldstone Builders is an owner-run residential builder in Lancaster, PA. We build homes with our own crews, and the work is on site. We are looking for a Master Carpenter to run the carpentry on our jobs, from layout and framing through finish work, and to help train the people coming up in the trade.

What the job involves
- Direct carpentry crews on site and assign daily tasks
- Train apprentices and check their work
- Keep the site safe and make sure everyone follows safety rules
- Order lumber and materials and track deliveries
- Read plans and lay out each job before work starts
- Check finished work against the plans before other trades come in

What you need
- 9 or more years of carpentry experience
- Rafter cutting, stair building and concrete forming
- Door and window installation, subfloor installation and cabinet installation
- Lead Carpenter Certificate (required)
- Crew Supervisor Certificate (preferred)
