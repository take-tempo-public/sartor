Master Carpenter
Fieldstone Builders — On site, Lancaster, PA

About us and the job
Fieldstone Builders is an owner-run residential builder in Lancaster, PA. We build and finish homes, and we care about work that is square, solid and done right the first time. We are looking for a master carpenter to run carpentry on our sites. This is on-site work every day, alongside our crews and apprentices.

What the job involves
- Direct carpentry crews and apprentices on site, set the day's work and check it before it is signed off
- Teach apprentices good habits with tools, layout and finish work
- Keep the site safe, tidy and in line with our safety rules, and stop work when something is not right
- Read plans, lay out the work and plan each job from start to finish
- Order lumber and materials so crews have what they need when they need it
- Talk with the owner and other trades to keep jobs on schedule

What you need
- 9 or more years of hands-on carpentry experience in residential building
- Rafter cutting, stair building and concrete forming done to plan without close supervision
- Door and window installation, subfloor installation and cabinet installation to a finished standard
- A current Construction Safety Card, which is required for this job
- A Crew Supervisor Certificate is preferred but not required
