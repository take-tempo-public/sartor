Master Electrician
Ironvale Industrial Contractors — On site, Houston, TX

Position summary
Ironvale Industrial Contractors seeks a Master Electrician to lead electrical work on industrial projects on site in Houston, TX. The position plans jobs, directs field crews and confirms that installations meet applicable codes and project specifications. Candidates must meet the minimum qualifications listed below. A shorter list of preferred qualifications will strengthen an application.

Responsibilities
- Pull and terminate wire for power, lighting and control circuits in industrial facilities.
- Plan jobs from drawings and specifications, sequencing work to meet project schedules.
- Order materials and coordinate deliveries so crews have what they need on site.
- Install panels, switchgear and fixtures according to drawings and applicable codes.
- Inspect completed work and direct helpers and apprentices on installation tasks.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Master Electrician Licence issued by the state, required.
- 9 or more years of electrical construction experience, required.
- Working knowledge of code compliance, load calculations and circuit testing, required.
- Experience with lighting installation in industrial settings, required.
- Experience with motor controls and fire alarm wiring, required.
