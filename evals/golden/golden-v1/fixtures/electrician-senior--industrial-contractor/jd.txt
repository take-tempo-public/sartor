Master Electrician
Ironvale Industrial Contractors — On site, Houston, TX

Position summary
Ironvale Industrial Contractors seeks a Master Electrician for on-site work in Houston, TX. The Master Electrician plans jobs, installs and tests electrical systems, and directs field crews to deliver safe, code-compliant work. Candidates must hold a master licence and have significant field experience. Preference is given to candidates with motor control and fire alarm experience.

Responsibilities
- Pull and terminate wire for power, lighting and control circuits to project specifications.
- Plan jobs from drawings, sequence work and coordinate schedules with site supervisors.
- Order materials and track stock so crews have what they need on site.
- Install panels, switchgear and fixtures in line with applicable codes.
- Test completed circuits and resolve faults before handover.
- Direct helpers and apprentices and check their work for quality and safety.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 9 or more years of electrical construction experience.
- Current Master Electrician Licence (required).
- Working knowledge of code compliance, load calculations and circuit testing.
- Hands-on experience with lighting installation and motor controls.
- Experience with fire alarm wiring (preferred).
