Master Electrician
Ironvale Industrial Contractors — On site, Houston, TX

Position summary
Ironvale Industrial Contractors seeks a Master Electrician for on-site work in Houston, TX. The successful candidate will plan and carry out industrial electrical installations, direct field crews, and ensure all work meets applicable codes. Minimum requirements include a master electrician licence, extensive field experience and sound knowledge of code compliance. Preference is given to candidates with experience in industrial motor controls.

Responsibilities
- Pull, terminate and label wire for power, lighting and control circuits.
- Plan jobs from drawings, estimate quantities and order materials ahead of each phase of work.
- Install, connect and test electrical panels and lighting fixtures.
- Direct helpers and apprentices and inspect their work for quality and code compliance.
- Coordinate with site supervisors on schedules, inspections and changes in scope.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 9 or more years of electrical construction experience, including work at the master level.
- Required certification: Apprentice Registration Card issued by the state.
- Demonstrated skill in code compliance, load calculations and circuit testing.
- Experience with lighting installation, motor controls and fire alarm wiring in industrial settings.
- Preferred: experience on large industrial sites in the Houston area.
