Senior HR Business Partner
Baxton Staffing Group — Hybrid, Phoenix, AZ

Position summary
Baxton Staffing Group seeks a Senior HR Business Partner to support managers and employees across a hybrid workforce based in Phoenix, AZ. The role partners with leaders on employee relations, hiring and benefits. Minimum: experience as an HR business partner, sound judgment on employee matters and clear written communication. Preferred: experience in a staffing company.

Responsibilities
- Screen candidates for open roles and coordinate next steps with hiring managers
- Advise managers on employee relations matters and recommend fair, consistent responses
- Answer employee benefits questions and explain coverage options clearly
- Guide managers through workplace concerns and document outcomes
- Partner with leaders on hiring plans and staffing needs

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 8 or more years of human resources experience
- Senior HR Practitioner Certificate (required)
- Experience with Workday and applicant tracking systems
- Experience running new hire onboarding, performance review cycles and exit interviews
- Working knowledge of employee relations practice
- Preferred: experience in a staffing company
