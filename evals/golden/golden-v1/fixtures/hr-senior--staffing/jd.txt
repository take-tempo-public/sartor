Senior HR Business Partner
Baxton Staffing Group — Hybrid, Phoenix, AZ

Position summary
Baxton Staffing Group seeks a Senior HR Business Partner to support managers across its hybrid operations in Phoenix, AZ. The role advises leaders on employee relations, guides hiring and onboarding, and keeps HR practices consistent across teams. Minimum qualifications include a bachelor's degree in human resources or a related field and prior experience as an HR business partner. Preferred qualifications include experience in a staffing environment.

Responsibilities
- Screen candidates for open roles and coordinate interviews with hiring managers.
- Advise managers on employee relations matters, including conduct, performance and workplace concerns.
- Answer employee benefits questions and refer complex cases to the appropriate benefits contact.
- Partner with department leaders on hiring plans and workforce needs.
- Keep HR records accurate, complete and current.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Required: 8 or more years of human resources experience, including employee relations work.
- Required certification: HR Practitioner Certificate.
- Required: Hands-on experience with Workday and applicant tracking systems.
- Required: Experience running new hire onboarding, exit interviews and performance review cycles.
- Preferred: Experience supporting managers in a staffing or recruiting environment.
