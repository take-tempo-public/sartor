Administrative Assistant
Whitcombe Logistics Group — On site, Memphis, TN

Position summary
Whitcombe Logistics Group is seeking an Administrative Assistant to support its on-site office in Memphis, TN. The role provides administrative support to the operations team of a freight company and keeps records, purchasing and correspondence accurate and on schedule.
Minimum: experience in office administration and working knowledge of standard office software.
Preferred: experience in a freight or logistics setting.

Responsibilities
- Process employee expense reports, checking receipts against company policy before submission for approval.
- Order office and operations supplies, track deliveries and keep stock at agreed levels.
- Take accurate notes at team and management meetings and circulate them to attendees promptly.
- Maintain organized physical and digital files for purchasing and administrative records.
- Serve as a point of contact for staff questions on administrative procedures.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 5 or more years of experience in an administrative support role.
- Demonstrated skill in vendor coordination, purchase ordering and invoice processing.
- Experience with visitor reception and handling incoming calls in a professional manner.
- Ability to prepare clear meeting minutes and distribute them on time.
- Proficiency with Microsoft Outlook for email and calendar management.
- Office Administration Certificate required.
