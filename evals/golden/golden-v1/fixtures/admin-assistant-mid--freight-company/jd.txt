Administrative Assistant
Whitcombe Logistics Group — On site, Memphis, TN

Position summary
Whitcombe Logistics Group seeks an Administrative Assistant to support daily office operations at its Memphis, TN location. This on-site role supports staff across the freight business with purchasing, records, reception and scheduling, and ensures that routine office requests are completed accurately and on time.
Minimum: prior office administration experience and working knowledge of Microsoft Office.
Preferred: experience in a freight or logistics setting.

Responsibilities
- Process employee expense reports, verify receipts and route claims for approval.
- Order office supplies, track deliveries and maintain adequate stock levels.
- Take, prepare and distribute meeting notes and follow up on action items.
- Coordinate with vendors on orders, pricing and billing questions.
- Maintain organized physical and electronic records for the office.
- Greet visitors and direct calls to the appropriate staff.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 5 or more years of administrative or office support experience.
- Demonstrated experience in vendor coordination, purchase ordering and invoice processing.
- Experience with visitor reception and front desk coverage.
- Ability to record and distribute accurate meeting minutes.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Outlook for email and calendar management.
- Office Administration Certificate required.
