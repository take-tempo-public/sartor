Customer Support Representative
Fairlane Subscription Box — Remote (US)

About the role
Fairlane Subscription Box is a small team that ships surprise boxes to subscribers every month, and our customers are the reason we exist. We're looking for a Customer Support Representative who likes solving problems, writes clearly and stays calm when a box goes missing. You'll be the friendly voice people reach when something isn't right, and you'll help us get better every time.

What you'll do
- Answer subscriber questions by chat, email and phone, and keep every reply friendly and clear
- Resolve complaints about late, damaged or missing boxes and follow each one through to a fix
- Document tickets carefully so anyone on the team can pick up a case without starting over
- Track customer satisfaction scores and flag trends the team should know about
- Pass recurring product and shipping issues to the right people with the details they need
- Suggest updates to our help content when the same questions keep coming in

What we're looking for
- 2 or more years of experience in customer support
- Escalation handling skills and a calm approach with frustrated customers
- Knowledge base writing that turns common questions into clear help articles
- Comfort with macros and templates, plus good judgment about when to write a reply from scratch
- Experience with satisfaction surveys and acting on what customers tell you
- Familiarity with refund processing and account verification
