Backend Engineer
Marlowe Retail Systems — Hybrid, Charlotte, NC

Position summary
Marlowe Retail Systems, a national retail company, seeks a Backend Engineer to design, build and maintain the services that support its retail operations. The engineer will work within a cross-functional team to keep these services reliable, efficient and well documented.
Minimum: experience building backend services in Python on Linux and supporting automated deployment pipelines.
Preferred: familiarity with Kafka and Kubernetes.

Responsibilities
- Maintain the reliability of production backend services and take part in a shared on-call rotation.
- Investigate and tune slow database queries to keep service response times within agreed targets.
- Automate and improve deployment pipelines so that releases are repeatable and low risk.
- Lead follow-up reviews after incidents and keep operational runbooks current.
- Collaborate with product and infrastructure teams to plan changes to services and data stores.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 4 or more years of professional experience in backend software engineering.
- Strong working knowledge of Python and of operating services on Linux.
- Experience building and maintaining CI pipelines in Jenkins.
- Experience managing infrastructure as code with Terraform.
- Practical experience with PostgreSQL, including query tuning and schema changes.
- Familiarity with API versioning practices and backward-compatible interface design.
