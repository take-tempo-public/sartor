Case Manager
Harvest Table Family Center — On site, Akron, OH

Summary
Harvest Table Family Center is a family services nonprofit that helps local families reach stability through housing, food and support services. We are seeking a Case Manager to work on site in Akron, OH, supporting families from intake through case closure. A current Mandated Reporter Certificate is required for this position.

Duties
- Conduct home visits to assess family safety, needs and progress toward goals
- Complete intake interviews with new families and gather required documents
- Carry out needs assessments and update them as family circumstances change
- Write service plans with clear goals, responsibilities and follow-up steps
- Coordinate referrals with schools, clinics and community partners
- Keep case records current and accurate

Licensure and qualifications
- 4 or more years of experience in case management or social services
- Experience with referral coordination, benefits enrollment and needs assessments
- Working knowledge of Microsoft Access for tracking case data
- Skill in community outreach and in writing clear, timely case notes
- Mandated Reporter Certificate required
- Human Services Supervisor Certificate preferred
