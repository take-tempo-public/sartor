Construction Manager
Quarry Hill Design-Build — Hybrid, Nashville, TN

About us and the job
Quarry Hill Design-Build is an owner-run design-build firm in Nashville. We design and build our projects under one contract, and we stay close to the work. As Construction Manager, you will spend most of your time on site, leading our project teams, meeting with owners and keeping jobs safe, on budget and on schedule. The role is hybrid, with office days in Nashville.

What the job involves
- Run regular owner meetings and report progress, cost and risks in plain terms.
- Manage our project managers and superintendents, setting priorities and checking work on site.
- Price change orders and review them with owners before work proceeds.
- Keep schedules and budgets current across active projects.
- Walk sites with superintendents to check safety, quality and progress.

What you need
- 11 or more years of construction project management experience.
- Working knowledge of Oracle Primavera and Microsoft Project for scheduling.
- Experience with permit management and reading models in Autodesk Revit.
- Solid subcontractor coordination and contract administration skills.
- Construction Safety Manager Certificate required.
