Construction Manager
Quarry Hill Design-Build — Hybrid, Nashville, TN

Quarry Hill Design-Build is an owner-run design-build firm. We design and build our projects under one roof, and our people do their work on site, not behind a desk. As Construction Manager, you will run our jobs in the field, keep owners informed, and lead the project managers and superintendents who get the work built. The role is hybrid and based in Nashville, Tennessee.

What the job involves
- Run regular owner meetings and keep owners informed on progress, cost and schedule
- Manage and support our project managers and superintendents
- Price change orders carefully and review them with owners before work proceeds
- Walk sites with superintendents to check quality, safety and progress
- Resolve field issues with designers, trades and owners

What you need
- 11 or more years of experience in construction project management
- Working knowledge of Oracle Primavera, Microsoft Project and Autodesk Revit
- Permit management and contract administration experience
- Subcontractor coordination across multiple trades
- Construction Safety Manager Certificate (required)
