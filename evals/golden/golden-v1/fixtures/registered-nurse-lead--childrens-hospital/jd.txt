Nurse Manager
Redstone Children's Hospital — On site, Louisville, KY

Summary
Redstone Children's Hospital exists to give every child in our community safe, family-centred care close to home. We are seeking a Nurse Manager to lead nursing staff on site in Louisville, KY, guide daily unit operations, and support families through each stay. A current Registered Nurse Licence and a Nurse Manager Certificate are required.

Duties
- Serve as charge nurse for assigned shifts, setting assignments and responding to changes in patient acuity
- Lead shift handoff at the bedside so oncoming nurses receive accurate, complete patient information
- Chart care, assessments and medications in the electronic record in a timely way
- Review staff charting for accuracy and follow up on gaps
- Coordinate with physicians and families on plans of care

Licensure and qualifications
- 12 or more years of nursing experience, including time in a leadership role
- Nurse Manager Certificate required, along with a current Registered Nurse Licence
- Demonstrated skill in bedside handoff, medication administration and IV therapy
- Experience in discharge planning with patients and families
- Working knowledge of infection control and patient assessment standards
