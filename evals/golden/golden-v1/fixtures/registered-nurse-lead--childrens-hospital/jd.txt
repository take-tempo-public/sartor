Nurse Manager
Redstone Children's Hospital — On site, Louisville, KY

Redstone Children's Hospital gives children and their families safe, compassionate care close to home. We are seeking an on-site Nurse Manager in Louisville, KY to lead nursing staff, oversee daily patient care and uphold clinical standards on the unit. A current Registered Nurse Licence is required, along with a current Basic Life Support Card.

Duties
- Serve as charge nurse for assigned shifts, directing staff assignments and patient flow
- Lead shift handoff so incoming nurses receive complete, accurate patient information
- Chart assessments, interventions and outcomes in the electronic health record in a timely way
- Review charting by nursing staff for accuracy and completeness
- Coordinate with physicians and families on plans of care
- Supervise and coach nursing staff and aides on the unit

Licensure and qualifications
- Current Registered Nurse Licence required
- 12 or more years of nursing experience, including leadership of a nursing team
- Skilled in bedside handoff, medication administration and IV therapy
- Experience with patient assessment and discharge planning for children and families
- Working knowledge of infection control practices and standards
