Support Team Lead
Summit Telecom Group — On site, Omaha, NE

Position summary
Summit Telecom Group seeks a Support Team Lead to guide its on-site support team in Omaha, NE. The Support Team Lead oversees daily support operations, coaches agents and is accountable for service quality across customer channels. Minimum requirements include team leadership experience and a working knowledge of support tools. Preference is given to candidates with telecommunications experience.

Responsibilities
- Review and update help articles so that published guidance reflects current products and common customer issues.
- Track customer satisfaction scores and report trends to management.
- Set support schedules that match expected contact volume across the week.
- Define team goals and review progress with each agent on a regular basis.
- Coach agents on case handling and identify areas for further training.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 10 or more years of experience in customer support, including time in a lead role.
- Escalation handling and ticket triage experience in a high-volume support environment.
- Working knowledge of Zendesk and Salesforce.
- Preferred: experience running satisfaction surveys and supporting customers through live chat.
