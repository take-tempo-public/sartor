HR Generalist
Clearwater Community Hospital — On site, Boise, ID

Summary
Clearwater Community Hospital provides compassionate, accessible care to every member of the community it serves. We are seeking an on-site HR Generalist in Boise, ID to support hiring, onboarding and employee records for our clinical and support staff. Candidates must hold the HR Practitioner Certificate.

Duties
- Schedule interviews between candidates and hiring managers across hospital departments.
- Screen applications and resumes against position requirements and forward qualified candidates.
- Maintain accurate and confidential HR records for all employees.
- Coordinate onboarding steps for new hires, including paperwork and orientation.
- Respond to employee questions about policies and procedures.
- Support compliance with hospital and regulatory HR requirements.

Licensure and qualifications
- HR Practitioner Certificate required; Senior HR Practitioner Certificate preferred.
- 4 or more years of experience in human resources.
- Working knowledge of applicant tracking, interview scheduling and new hire onboarding.
- Experience using Workday for employee records and HR processes.
- Familiarity with performance review cycles and benefits administration.
