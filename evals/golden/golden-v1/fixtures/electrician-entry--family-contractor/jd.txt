Apprentice Electrician
Hollis and Sons Electric — On site, Dayton, OH

Hollis and Sons Electric is a family electrical contractor, owned and run by the family in Dayton, Ohio. We are hiring an Apprentice Electrician to work on site alongside our journeymen. You will learn the trade on real jobs, help with installs and repairs, and have your work checked before we close out each job. We treat our crew well and ask for care, honesty and steady effort in return.

What the job involves
- Trace faults in circuits and equipment alongside a journeyman
- Read plans and drawings to lay out each job before work starts
- Install panels and mount them to plan
- Set and wire light fixtures, outlets and switches
- Run conduit and pull wire on site
- Keep the work area clean and safe at the end of each day

What you need
- 2 or more years of experience in electrical work
- Panel installation and troubleshooting skills on site
- Blueprint reading and multimeter use
- Fire alarm wiring and grounding and bonding
- A current Apprentice Registration Card is required
- A Journeyman Electrician Licence is preferred
