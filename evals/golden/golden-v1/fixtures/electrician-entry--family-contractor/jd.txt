Apprentice Electrician
Hollis and Sons Electric — On site, Dayton, OH

Hollis and Sons Electric is a family electrical contractor in Dayton, Ohio. We are owner-run, and the owners still work on the job sites with the crew. We are hiring an Apprentice Electrician to work on site alongside a journeyman and learn the trade on real jobs. You will spend your days at customer sites, not in a shop, as part of a small team that looks out for each other.

What the job involves
- Trace faults in circuits and equipment with a journeyman and help find the cause
- Read plans and drawings to lay out the work before it starts
- Install panels and connect circuits to them
- Install light fixtures, switches and outlets to the plans
- Keep the work area clean and safe at the end of each day

What you need
- 2 or more years of experience in electrical work
- Hands-on skill in Panel installation and Troubleshooting
- Blueprint reading and Multimeter use on the job
- Experience with Fire alarm wiring, and Grounding and bonding
- A current Apprentice Registration Card (required)
- A Journeyman Electrician Licence (preferred, not required)
