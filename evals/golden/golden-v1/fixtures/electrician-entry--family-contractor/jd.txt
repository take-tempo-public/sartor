Apprentice Electrician
Hollis and Sons Electric — On site, Dayton, OH

About us and the job
Hollis and Sons Electric is a family-owned electrical contractor in Dayton, Ohio. We run the business ourselves, know our customers by name and stand behind every job we do. We are looking for an apprentice electrician to work on site alongside our licensed electricians. You will learn the trade by doing the work, with someone experienced checking it before we hand it over.

What the job involves
- Tracing faults in circuits and equipment and helping find the cause before repairs begin
- Reading plans and drawings to lay out each job before work starts
- Installing panels, breakers and fixtures to the drawings and to code
- Pulling, terminating and labelling wire neatly so the next person can follow it
- Keeping the job site tidy and safe and looking after tools and materials
- Asking questions and having your work checked by a licensed electrician

What you need
- 2 or more years of experience as an electrical apprentice or helper
- Panel installation and fire alarm wiring on site
- Troubleshooting with confident multimeter use
- Blueprint reading and a working knowledge of grounding and bonding
- A current Construction Safety Card (required)
- A Journeyman Electrician Licence is preferred but not required
