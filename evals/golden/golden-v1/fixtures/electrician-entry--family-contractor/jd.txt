Apprentice Electrician
Hollis and Sons Electric — On site, Dayton, OH

About us and the job
Hollis and Sons Electric is a family-owned electrical contractor in Dayton, Ohio. The owners still work on the tools and know every customer by name. We are looking for an apprentice electrician to join our crew on site. You will work alongside a journeyman, learn the trade the right way, and help us finish jobs that we are proud to put our name on.

What the job involves
- Trace faults in circuits and equipment with a journeyman and help find the cause
- Read plans and blueprints to lay out runs, boxes and fixtures before work starts
- Install panels and connect circuits to them under supervision
- Mount and wire light fixtures, switches and outlets
- Keep the work area clean and safe, and put tools and materials away at the end of the day
- Ask questions and have your work checked before it is closed up

What you need
- Panel installation and fire alarm wiring experience
- Troubleshooting skills, including multimeter use
- Blueprint reading and a working knowledge of grounding and bonding
- 2 or more years of experience in electrical work
- Construction Safety Card, required
- Journeyman Electrician Licence, preferred
