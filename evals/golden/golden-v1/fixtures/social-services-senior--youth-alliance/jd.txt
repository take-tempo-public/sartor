Senior Case Manager
Crossroads Youth and Family Alliance — On site, Indianapolis, IN

Summary
Crossroads Youth and Family Alliance helps young people and their families find stability, safety and support. We are seeking a Senior Case Manager to lead complex casework, guide fellow case managers and connect families with the services they need. A Case Management Certificate is required for this role.

Duties
- Keep accurate and timely case records for all assigned youth and families.
- Refer families to housing and food programs and follow up to confirm services were received.
- Manage program budgets and track spending against approved plans.
- Prepare program reports for leadership and funders.
- Guide case managers on documentation standards and complex cases.

Licensure and qualifications
- Case Management Certificate required.
- Human Services Supervisor Certificate preferred.
- 9 or more years of experience in case management or social services.
- Skill in caseload tracking and Microsoft Access.
- Experience with motivational interviewing and crisis intervention.
- Background in benefits enrollment and community outreach.
