Senior Case Manager
Crossroads Youth and Family Alliance — On site, Indianapolis, IN

Summary
Crossroads Youth and Family Alliance helps young people and their families build safe, stable lives. We are seeking a Senior Case Manager to carry complex casework, support case management staff and coordinate services with community partners. This is an on-site position in Indianapolis, IN. A current Mandated Reporter Certificate is required for this role.

Duties
- Maintain accurate, timely case records for each youth and family on the caseload
- Refer families to housing and food programs and follow up to confirm services are in place
- Manage program budgets and track spending against approved plans
- Prepare program reports for leadership and funders
- Review case files with staff and help resolve difficult cases

Licensure and qualifications
- 9 or more years of experience in case management or social services
- Mandated Reporter Certificate required
- Human Services Supervisor Certificate preferred
- Working skill in caseload tracking and Microsoft Access
- Training in motivational interviewing and crisis intervention
- Experience with benefits enrollment and community outreach
