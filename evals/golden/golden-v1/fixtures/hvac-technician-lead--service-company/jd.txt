HVAC Service Lead
Bellwether Comfort Services — On site, Omaha, NE

About us and the job
Bellwether Comfort Services is an owner-run heating and cooling company serving homes and businesses across the region. We are hiring an HVAC Service Lead to run our service crew on site in Omaha, NE. You will take the hard calls, guide our technicians, and make sure every job is done right before the van leaves.

What the job involves
- Lead the service crew on site and answer for the quality of every job
- Keep work orders accurate and up to date from first call to final invoice
- Train apprentices on safe, correct repair and installation work
- Test systems for leaks and confirm repairs before closing a job
- Explain findings and repair options to customers in plain terms

What you need
- 15 or more years of experience in heating and cooling service
- Hands-on skill in building automation and electrical testing
- Experience with duct installation and leak testing
- Sound knowledge of heat pump service and system diagnostics
- A current Refrigerant Compliance Certificate (required)
