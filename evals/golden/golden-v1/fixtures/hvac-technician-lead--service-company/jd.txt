HVAC Service Lead
Bellwether Comfort Services — On site, Omaha, NE

About us and the job
Bellwether Comfort Services is an owner-run heating and cooling company serving homes and businesses around Omaha, NE. We are looking for an HVAC Service Lead to run our service crew on site, take on the harder calls and make sure every job is done right the first time. You will work directly with the owners and our customers, and your word will carry weight on how the work gets done.

What the job involves
- Lead the service crew on site and take charge of the more difficult calls
- Keep work orders accurate and complete, from first call to final sign-off
- Train apprentices on the job and check their work before it is closed out
- Test systems for leaks after repairs and installations and make sure faults are fixed
- Explain findings and repair options to customers in plain words
- Keep tools, parts and vans ready so the crew can work without delays

What you need
- 15 or more years of heating and cooling service experience
- Hands-on skill in building automation, electrical testing and system diagnostics
- Experience with duct installation, leak testing and heat pump service
- Controls Technician Certificate, required
- Ability to lead a crew and answer for the finished job
