Sales Development Representative
Brightside Office Supply — Hybrid, Louisville, KY

About us and the job
Brightside Office Supply is a regional office-supply distributor, owned and run by the people who started it. We help local businesses keep their offices stocked and running. We are hiring a Sales Development Representative to find new customers and get them set up with us. The job is hybrid, with regular days working alongside the team at our Louisville office.

What the job involves
- Find and contact businesses in our area that could become new accounts
- Talk with prospects to learn what they order and how often
- Prepare quotes and help put together contracts with the owner and sales team
- Keep track of every prospect and follow up on time
- Hand new customers over to our customer success team with clear notes so nothing is missed

What you need
- 2 or more years of experience in business-to-business sales
- Cold outreach by phone and email, and comfort using Sales Navigator to find prospects
- Product demonstrations that are clear and focused on what the customer needs
- Account planning and pipeline management for a steady list of prospects
- Sales forecasting that gives the owner an honest view of what is coming in
