Journeyman Carpenter
Rowan Remodeling — On site, Eugene, OR

About us and the job
Rowan Remodeling is an owner-run remodeling contractor in Eugene, OR. We rework kitchens, baths and other rooms, and we take care to do the job right the first time. We are looking for a journeyman carpenter to work on site with our crew, handling carpentry from framing through finish. You will work directly with the owner and always know who to go to with a question.

What the job involves
- Keep the site safe, clean and organized for the crew and the homeowner
- Order lumber and materials for each job and check deliveries against the order
- Frame walls and floors for remodels and additions
- Read plans and lay out the work before cutting
- Work with the owner and crew to keep each job on schedule

What you need
- 4 or more years of carpentry experience, including framing and stair building
- Concrete forming and door and window installation skills
- Blueprint reading and accurate layout
- Jobsite safety knowledge and steady work habits
- Apprenticeship Completion Certificate (required)
- Lead Carpenter Certificate (preferred)
