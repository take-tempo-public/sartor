Journeyman Carpenter
Rowan Remodeling — On site, Eugene, OR

About us and the job
Rowan Remodeling is an owner-run remodeling contractor in Eugene, OR. We remodel homes for local families and stand behind every job we finish. We are looking for a journeyman carpenter to join our crew. This job is on site every day, working alongside the owner and the rest of the team. We trust our carpenters to do careful work and to treat each client's home with respect.

What the job involves
- Keep the site safe for the crew and the homeowner, and clean up at the end of each day
- Order lumber and materials ahead of time so the work keeps moving
- Frame walls and floors to plan, checking that everything is level, plumb and square
- Read the plans and confirm measurements before cutting
- Work with the owner to plan each stage of the remodel

What you need
- 4 or more years of experience as a carpenter
- Hands-on skill in framing, stair building and concrete forming
- Experience with door and window installation
- Strong blueprint reading and careful measuring
- Good jobsite safety habits
- A State Apprenticeship Completion Certificate is required; a State Lead Carpenter Certificate is preferred
