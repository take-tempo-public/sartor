Journeyman Carpenter
Rowan Remodeling — On site, Eugene, OR

About us and the job
Rowan Remodeling is a remodeling contractor run by its owner. We take care with every job and treat each client's home with respect. We are looking for a journeyman carpenter to join our crew. The work is on site in Eugene, and you will work alongside the owner and the rest of the crew from start to finish.

What the job involves
- Keep the site safe, clean and organised for the crew, clients and other trades.
- Order lumber and materials from the plans and check deliveries against the order.
- Frame walls and floors to plan, keeping them square, level and plumb.
- Read plans and take accurate measurements before cutting and building.
- Work with the owner to plan each day's tasks and keep jobs on schedule.

What you need
- 4 or more years of experience as a carpenter.
- Hands-on skill in framing, stair building and concrete forming.
- Experience with door and window installation.
- Blueprint reading and careful measuring.
- Good jobsite safety habits, with a current Construction Safety Card (required).
- A Lead Carpenter Certificate is preferred.
