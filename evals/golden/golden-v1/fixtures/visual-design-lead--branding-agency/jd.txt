Creative Director
Marigold Creative House — Hybrid, Austin, TX

Marigold Creative House is an independent branding agency, run by its owners and built on close work with clients. We are looking for a Creative Director to lead our design team, shape how our clients look in print and online, and keep the work clear and well made. Most of the work happens on site at our Austin studio, with some days from home.

What the job involves
- Lead the design team on print layouts for brochures, packaging and signage
- Present concepts to clients and manage client revisions through to sign-off
- Plan and run campaign launches across print and digital channels
- Review proofs and work with printers on final production
- Keep each client's brand guidelines up to date and applied consistently

What you need
- 11 or more years of experience in graphic or brand design
- A strong grounding in color theory and typography
- Skill in photo retouching using Adobe Photoshop
- Hands-on knowledge of print production, from file preparation to press checks
- Working knowledge of Adobe After Effects for motion work
