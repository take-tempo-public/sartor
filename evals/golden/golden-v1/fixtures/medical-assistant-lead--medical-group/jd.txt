Clinic Supervisor
Stonehaven Medical Group — On site, Savannah, GA

Summary
Stonehaven Medical Group exists to provide accessible, coordinated care to every patient who walks through our doors. The Clinic Supervisor leads the medical assistant team at our on-site Savannah, GA clinic, keeping patient flow, supplies and schedules running smoothly across the day. A current Certified Medical Assistant Credential is required for this position.

Duties
- Manage clinic supplies, reorder stock and track inventory across exam rooms and procedure areas.
- Build and adjust staff schedules to match patient volume and provider availability.
- Make sure exam rooms are stocked, cleaned and ready before each clinic session.
- Take and record vital signs, and coach medical assistants on accurate technique and documentation.
- Serve as the point of contact for staffing gaps, supply issues and patient flow concerns during clinic hours.

Licensure and qualifications
- Certified Medical Assistant Credential required.
- 13 or more years of medical assisting experience, including time leading a clinic team.
- Hands-on skill with injections and blood draws.
- Working knowledge of Epic Systems for charting, patient messaging and scheduling.
- Experience handling prior authorizations with insurers.
- Comfort managing patient intake and rooming in a busy outpatient setting.
