Clinic Supervisor
Stonehaven Medical Group — On site, Savannah, GA

Stonehaven Medical Group brings steady, accessible care to every neighbourhood it serves. We are seeking a Clinic Supervisor to lead daily operations at our on-site clinic in Savannah, GA, guiding medical assistants and supporting providers across a busy patient schedule within our multi-clinic group. A current Phlebotomy Certificate is required for this position.

Duties
- Manage clinic supplies, ordering and inventory so care is never delayed by missing items
- Build and adjust staff and patient schedules to keep clinic flow steady
- Stock exam rooms before each session and check them between visits
- Take vital signs and confirm accurate entry in the patient record
- Guide and support medical assistants through daily clinic tasks

Licensure and qualifications
- Phlebotomy Certificate required
- 13 or more years of experience in medical assisting or clinic operations
- Proficiency with injections and blood draws
- Working knowledge of Epic Systems, including patient messaging
- Experience handling prior authorizations and patient intake
