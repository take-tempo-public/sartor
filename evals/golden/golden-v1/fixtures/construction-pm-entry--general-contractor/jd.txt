Assistant Project Manager
Garrity Construction Group — On site, Charlotte, NC

Position summary
Garrity Construction Group, a general contractor, seeks an Assistant Project Manager to support project managers on site in Charlotte, NC. The role coordinates owner meetings, submittal reviews and site safety walks from preconstruction through closeout. At minimum, candidates bring field coordination experience and working knowledge of scheduling software. A degree in construction management is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Prepare agendas, minutes and action items for owner meetings.
- Review submittals against contract documents and track responses with the design team.
- Conduct site safety walks with superintendents and document corrective actions.
- Maintain project logs, requests for information and change order records.
- Coordinate subcontractor schedules and support permit and inspection requests.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 2 or more years of experience in construction project management with a general contractor.
- Working knowledge of Procore and Oracle Primavera.
- Schedule recovery, cost tracking and submittal tracking on active projects.
- Permit management and coordination of inspections with local authorities.
- Preferred: Construction Safety Manager Certificate.
