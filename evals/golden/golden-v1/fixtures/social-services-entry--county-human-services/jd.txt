Case Aide
Fairmont County Human Services — On site, Fairmont, OH

Fairmont County Human Services is seeking a Case Aide to support caseworkers who serve county residents and families. Under close supervision, the Case Aide meets with residents at intake, gathers information for assessments, connects families with community resources and keeps accurate case records. This is an on-site position in Fairmont, OH.

Responsibilities of the position
- Conduct intake interviews with residents applying for county services
- Complete needs assessments and record findings in the case file
- Refer families to housing, food and other community programs, and follow up to confirm services were received
- Prepare court reports and case summaries for hearings under the direction of an assigned caseworker
- Maintain accurate case records and protect client confidentiality

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 1 or more years of experience in social services casework or a related field
- Working knowledge of needs assessments, benefits enrollment and referral coordination
- Ability to prepare court reports and conduct home visits in the community
- Skill in motivational interviewing with individuals and families
- Required: a current Mandated Reporter Certificate
- Preferred: a Case Management Certificate
