Case Aide
Fairmont County Human Services — On site, Fairmont, OH

Duties
Fairmont County Human Services is seeking a Case Aide to support caseworkers in serving individuals and families across the county. Working on site in Fairmont under the direction of a casework supervisor, the Case Aide helps residents through intake, assessment and referral, keeps accurate case records, and supports staff in preparing documentation for hearings. The position requires steady judgment, respect for client confidentiality and clear communication with families and partner agencies.

Responsibilities of the position
- Conduct intake interviews with individuals and families requesting services.
- Complete needs assessments and record findings in the case file.
- Prepare reports for hearings in coordination with the assigned caseworker.
- Refer families to housing and food programs and follow up on the status of each referral.
- Maintain accurate, confidential case records and update them after each contact.
- Assist caseworkers with service planning and scheduling client appointments.

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 1 or more years of experience in human services casework or a related field.
- Working knowledge of needs assessments, benefits enrollment and referral coordination.
- Ability to prepare court reports and to conduct home visits throughout the county.
- Skill in motivational interviewing with individuals and families.
- Mandated Reporter Certificate required at the time of hire.
- Case Management Certificate preferred.
