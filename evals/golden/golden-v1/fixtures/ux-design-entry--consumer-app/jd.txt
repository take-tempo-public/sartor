Junior UX Designer
Fernwood Apps — Remote (US)

Fernwood Apps builds consumer apps that people actually enjoy opening. We're a small, remote team, and we're looking for a junior UX designer who wants to learn fast, ship often and care about the details. You'll work closely with a senior designer, take on well-scoped pieces of the product and see your work in users' hands quickly.

What you'll do
- Sketch and refine wireframes for new screens and flows in our app
- Find and fix accessibility issues, from colour contrast to screen reader labels
- Help plan and run usability sessions with real users
- Turn session notes into clear, prioritized design changes
- Share your work in regular design critiques and iterate on the feedback

What we're looking for
- 1 or more years of experience in product or UX design
- A solid grounding in interaction design and information architecture
- Comfort working in Figma, including prototyping clickable flows
- Hands-on user research experience, from interviews to usability sessions
- Working knowledge of HTML and CSS so you can collaborate easily with engineers
