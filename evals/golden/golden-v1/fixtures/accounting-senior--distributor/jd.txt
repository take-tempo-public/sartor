Senior Accountant
Davenport Distribution — Hybrid, Indianapolis, IN

Position summary
Davenport Distribution, a wholesale distributor, seeks a Senior Accountant for its hybrid Indianapolis, IN office. The position supports accurate and timely financial reporting, including the close, account reconciliations and audit work.
Minimum: a bachelor's degree in accounting and experience with general ledger work.
Preferred: experience in wholesale distribution.

Responsibilities
- Review vendor invoices for accuracy, coding and approval before payment.
- Own the close calendar for the accounting team and track each task to completion.
- Prepare schedules, reconciliations and supporting documents for the external audit.
- Respond to auditor requests and coordinate follow-up with other departments.
- Review journal entries prepared by staff accountants.
- Maintain documentation of close procedures and controls.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 7 or more years of accounting experience.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and experience with Accounts receivable.
- Hands-on experience with Month-end close and Account reconciliation.
- Experience providing Audit support and handling Sales tax filing.
- Bookkeeping Certificate required.
- Preferred: experience in wholesale distribution.
