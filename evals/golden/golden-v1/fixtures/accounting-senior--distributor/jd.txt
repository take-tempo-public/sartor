Senior Accountant
Davenport Distribution — Hybrid, Indianapolis, IN

Position summary
Davenport Distribution, a wholesale distributor, seeks a Senior Accountant to support the accuracy and timeliness of its financial reporting. The position works with operations and finance leadership to maintain sound controls across payables, receivables and the monthly close. Minimum qualifications include a bachelor's degree in accounting, close experience and strong spreadsheet skills. Preferred qualifications include public accounting licensure and distribution industry experience.

Responsibilities
- Review vendor invoices for accuracy, approval and proper coding before payment.
- Own the close calendar for the accounting team, assigning tasks and monitoring deadlines.
- Lead audit preparation, including assembling schedules and responding to auditor requests.
- Review account reconciliations prepared by staff and resolve open items.
- Prepare monthly financial reporting for management review.
- Recommend process improvements that strengthen controls and reduce manual effort.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 7 or more years of accounting experience, including month-end close ownership.
- Advanced Microsoft Excel skills for reconciliations, schedules and reporting.
- Working knowledge of accounts receivable and account reconciliation processes.
- Experience with sales tax filing across multiple jurisdictions.
- Background in audit support, including preparing documentation for external auditors.
- Required: Certified Public Accountant Licence.
