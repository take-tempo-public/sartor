Senior Accountant
Davenport Distribution — Hybrid, Indianapolis, IN

Position summary
Davenport Distribution, a wholesale distributor, seeks a Senior Accountant to support accurate and timely financial reporting. The Senior Accountant reviews invoices, maintains the close calendar for the accounting team and leads preparation for audits. Minimum requirements include accounting experience, working knowledge of the month-end close and proficiency in Microsoft Excel. Experience with QuickBooks and fixed asset tracking is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Review vendor invoices for accuracy, proper approval and correct coding before payment.
- Own the close calendar for the accounting team, assigning tasks and tracking deadlines through each period.
- Prepare schedules, reconciliations and supporting documentation for internal and external audits.
- Respond to auditor requests and coordinate follow-up items with the team.
- Review journal entries and account balances prepared by staff accountants.
- Recommend process improvements that strengthen controls and shorten the close.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 7 or more years of accounting experience, including month-end close responsibilities.
- Advanced skills in Microsoft Excel for reconciliations, schedules and reporting.
- Working knowledge of accounts receivable and account reconciliation practices.
- Experience providing audit support and preparing documentation for auditors.
- Experience with sales tax filing for a multi-location business.
- Certified Public Accountant Licence required.
