Senior Accountant
Davenport Distribution — Hybrid, Indianapolis, IN

Position summary
Davenport Distribution, a wholesale distributor, seeks a Senior Accountant to support accurate and timely financial reporting from its hybrid Indianapolis, IN office. The Senior Accountant will review invoices, manage the team's close calendar and prepare the company for audits. Minimum requirements include accounting experience, close experience and strong spreadsheet skills. Experience in wholesale distribution is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Review vendor and customer invoices for accuracy, coding and approval before posting.
- Own the close calendar for the accounting team, setting deadlines and tracking completion of each task.
- Prepare schedules, reconciliations and supporting documents for internal and external audits.
- Respond to auditor requests and coordinate follow-up items with the accounting team.
- Review journal entries and account balances prepared by staff accountants.
- Recommend improvements to close and review procedures.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 7 or more years of accounting experience, including month-end close and account reconciliation.
- Working knowledge of accounts receivable and sales tax filing.
- Advanced skills in Microsoft Excel.
- Experience providing audit support to external auditors.
- Bookkeeping Certificate required.
- Preferred: experience in wholesale distribution.
