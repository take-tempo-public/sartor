Staff Accountant
Redwood Tax and Bookkeeping — On site, Sacramento, CA

About us and the job
Redwood Tax and Bookkeeping is a small, owner-run accounting firm in Sacramento, California. We keep the books, prepare taxes and help local businesses stay on top of their finances. We are hiring a Staff Accountant to join our team. The job is on site in our office, and you will work closely with the owners and our small staff on client accounts.

What the job involves
- Prepare monthly financial statements for our small business clients
- Review vendor invoices for accuracy, coding and approval before payment
- Reconcile client bank and credit card accounts and clear any differences
- Post journal entries and adjustments as needed
- Help with month-end close and keep client files organised

What you need
- 1 or more years of experience in accounting or bookkeeping
- Working knowledge of journal entries, month-end close and budget variance analysis
- Experience with fixed asset tracking and sales tax filing
- Comfort working in SAP
- Bookkeeping Certificate (required)
- Certified Public Accountant Licence (preferred)
