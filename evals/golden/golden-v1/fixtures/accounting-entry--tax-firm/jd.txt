Staff Accountant
Redwood Tax and Bookkeeping — On site, Sacramento, CA

About us and the job
Redwood Tax and Bookkeeping is a small, owner-run accounting firm in Sacramento, CA. We look after the books and tax work for local businesses and families, and we know our clients by name. We are hiring a Staff Accountant to join our team in the office. This job is on site, and you will work closely with the owners and the rest of our small staff every day.

What the job involves
- Prepare monthly financial statements for our business clients
- Review vendor invoices for accuracy, coding and approval before payment
- Complete bank reconciliations and follow up on any differences
- Post adjustments and keep client ledgers up to date
- Help gather records and answer client questions during busy periods
- Work with the owners to make sure files are complete and ready for review

What you need
- 1 or more years of experience in an accounting or bookkeeping role
- Working knowledge of journal entries, month-end close and budget variance analysis
- Hands-on experience with fixed asset tracking and sales tax filing
- Familiarity with SAP
- Bookkeeping Certificate (required)
- Certified Public Accountant Licence (preferred)
