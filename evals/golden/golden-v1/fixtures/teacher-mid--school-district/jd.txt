Classroom Teacher
Ashgrove Unified School District — On site, Ashgrove, OH

Duties
Ashgrove Unified School District is seeking a Classroom Teacher to plan and deliver daily instruction in an assigned elementary classroom. The teacher maintains a safe and orderly learning environment, assesses student progress, and adjusts instruction to meet individual needs. The position works closely with families, building staff, and grade-level colleagues, and follows district curriculum and policies.

Responsibilities of the position
- Prepare for and lead parent conferences, sharing student progress and agreeing on next steps with families.
- Plan and teach small-group reading lessons matched to student needs.
- Take part in team planning with grade-level colleagues to align lessons, pacing and assessments.
- Monitor student progress and use the results to adjust instruction.
- Maintain clear classroom routines and accurate student records.

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 4 or more years of experience as an elementary classroom teacher.
- Demonstrated skill in Classroom management and Parent communication.
- Experience with Differentiated instruction and Math manipulatives.
- Working knowledge of Phonics instruction and Reading intervention.
- Required: a current State Teaching Licence.
- Preferred: Instructional Leadership Endorsement.
