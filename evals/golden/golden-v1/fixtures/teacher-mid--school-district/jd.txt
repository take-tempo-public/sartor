Classroom Teacher
Ashgrove Unified School District — On site, Ashgrove, OH

Duties
Ashgrove Unified School District is seeking a Classroom Teacher to join an elementary school team in Ashgrove, Ohio. Under the general direction of the building principal, the teacher plans and delivers instruction aligned with the district curriculum, keeps a safe and orderly classroom, monitors student progress, and works with families and staff to support student learning. This is an on-site position.

Responsibilities of the position
- Plan and lead small-group reading sessions matched to student needs and reading levels.
- Hold parent conferences to review student progress, share work samples and agree on next steps.
- Take part in team planning with grade-level colleagues to align units, materials and assessments.
- Monitor student progress through ongoing classroom checks and adjust instruction based on results.
- Maintain clear classroom routines and expectations that support a safe learning environment.
- Communicate regularly with families about classroom activities and student growth.

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 4 or more years of experience as an elementary classroom teacher.
- Demonstrated classroom management and parent communication skills.
- Experience using differentiated instruction and math manipulatives in daily lessons.
- Working knowledge of phonics instruction and reading intervention practices.
- Required: a valid State Teaching Licence.
- Preferred: Instructional Leadership Endorsement.
