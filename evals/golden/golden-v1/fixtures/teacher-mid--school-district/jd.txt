Classroom Teacher
Ashgrove Unified School District — On site, Ashgrove, OH

Ashgrove Unified School District is seeking a Classroom Teacher to join an on-site elementary team in Ashgrove. The teacher plans and delivers daily instruction, assesses student progress, maintains a safe and orderly classroom, and works with families and colleagues to support each student's learning. The position reports to the building principal and follows district curriculum and policies.

Responsibilities of the position
- Plan and deliver daily lessons aligned with district curriculum
- Lead small-group reading instruction based on student assessment results
- Hold parent conferences and keep families informed of student progress
- Take part in team planning with grade-level colleagues and support staff
- Monitor student progress and adjust instruction as needed

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 4 or more years of experience as an elementary classroom teacher
- Demonstrated skill in classroom management and parent communication
- Experience with differentiated instruction and the use of math manipulatives
- Working knowledge of phonics instruction and reading intervention
- Required: a valid Reading Specialist Endorsement issued by the state
- Preferred: Instructional Leadership Endorsement
