Classroom Teacher
Ashgrove Unified School District — On site, Ashgrove, OH

The Ashgrove Unified School District seeks a Classroom Teacher for on-site work at a district school in Ashgrove. Under the general direction of the building principal, the Classroom Teacher plans and delivers instruction for an assigned class, keeps a safe and orderly learning environment, assesses student progress, and keeps families informed. The position works closely with grade-level colleagues and school support staff.

Responsibilities of the position
- Plan and deliver daily lessons aligned with district curriculum standards.
- Lead small-group reading sessions that match texts and tasks to each student's current level.
- Hold parent conferences to review student progress, set goals and agree on next steps.
- Take part in team planning with grade-level colleagues to align lessons, assessments and classroom routines.
- Assess student learning regularly and adjust instruction based on the results.
- Keep accurate records of attendance, grades and family contacts.

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- At least 4 years of experience as a classroom teacher.
- Classroom management and parent communication skills that support a calm, well-run classroom and steady contact with families.
- Differentiated instruction, including the use of math manipulatives to teach concepts.
- Phonics instruction and reading intervention for students who need extra support.
- Reading Specialist Endorsement required.
- Instructional Leadership Endorsement preferred.
