Journeyman Electrician
Marlow Residential Electric — On site, Spokane, WA

Marlow Residential Electric is an owner-run electrical contractor doing residential work in and around Spokane. We are looking for a journeyman electrician to join our crew on site. You will wire homes, handle service work and do the job to code, keeping each site clean and safe. We keep crews small, treat people fairly and stand behind our work.

What the job involves
- Read plans to lay out each job before work starts
- Pull and terminate wire in new construction and remodels
- Install panels, breakers and subpanels
- Mount and connect fixtures, switches and receptacles
- Test circuits and fix problems before inspection
- Keep the work area clean, organised and safe

What you need
- 5 or more years of hands-on electrical experience
- Solid skills in grounding and bonding, circuit testing and troubleshooting
- Code compliance knowledge and confident blueprint reading
- Ability to do load calculations for residential services
- Apprentice Registration Card required
- Master Electrician Licence preferred
