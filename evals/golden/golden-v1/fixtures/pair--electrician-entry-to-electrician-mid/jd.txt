Journeyman Electrician
Marlow Residential Electric — On site, Spokane, WA

About us and the job
Marlow Residential Electric is an owner-run electrical contractor in Spokane, WA, wiring homes for local families and builders. We are looking for a journeyman electrician who takes pride in neat, safe work. The job is on site every day, in new builds, remodels and service calls. You will work closely with the owner and a small crew.

What the job involves
- Read plans and drawings to lay out each job before work starts
- Pull wire through new and existing homes and terminate it cleanly at devices and panels
- Install and upgrade service panels, breakers and subpanels
- Hang and connect light fixtures, fans, switches and receptacles
- Test finished circuits and get work ready for inspection
- Keep the job site clean and talk clearly with homeowners

What you need
- 5 or more years of hands-on electrical experience
- Grounding and bonding, circuit testing and code compliance on residential jobs
- Blueprint reading and load calculations for new and remodel work
- Troubleshooting skills to find and fix faults on your own
- A current Journeyman Electrician Licence is required
- A Master Electrician Licence is preferred
