Journeyman Electrician
Marlow Residential Electric — On site, Spokane, WA

About us and the job
Marlow Residential Electric is an owner-run electrical contractor working in homes in and around Spokane. We wire new houses, update older ones and fix what has gone wrong. We are looking for a journeyman electrician to join our crew. The work is on site, in customers' homes, and you will often run a job on your own. We care about doing the work right and treating our people and customers fairly.

What the job involves
- Read plans and drawings to lay out each job before you start
- Pull and terminate wire in new builds and existing homes
- Install and upgrade panels, breakers and service equipment
- Mount and connect light fixtures, outlets, switches and fans
- Test your work and leave each site clean and ready for inspection
- Talk with homeowners about what you are doing and why

What you need
- 5 or more years of experience as an electrician, mostly in residential work
- A current Journeyman Electrician Licence is required; a Master Electrician Licence is preferred
- Solid skills in grounding and bonding, circuit testing and code compliance
- Blueprint reading and load calculations for residential services and circuits
- Troubleshooting skills to find and fix faults in existing wiring
