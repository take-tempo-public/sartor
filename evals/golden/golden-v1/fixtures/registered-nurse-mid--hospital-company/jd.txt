Registered Nurse
Summit Health Partners — On site, Charlotte, NC

Position summary
Summit Health Partners, a multi-state hospital company, seeks a Registered Nurse to provide safe, patient-centred care on site in Charlotte, NC. The Registered Nurse assesses patients, administers medications, educates patients and families, and maintains accurate records in collaboration with the care team. Minimum requirements include an active Registered Nurse licence, current life support certification and acute care experience. Preferred candidates will also hold a nursing leadership credential.

Responsibilities
- Educate patients and families on diagnoses, medications, self-care and discharge instructions, confirming understanding before discharge.
- Chart assessments, interventions and patient responses accurately and promptly in the electronic health record.
- Administer medications safely in accordance with physician orders, hospital policy and established verification practices.
- Monitor patients for medication effects and report adverse reactions to the responsible provider.
- Collaborate with physicians and the interdisciplinary team to plan, deliver and adjust patient care.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Required: 4 or more years of experience as a Registered Nurse.
- Required: Proficiency in Epic Systems and clinical documentation within the electronic health record.
- Required: Demonstrated skill in triage, patient assessment and care planning.
- Required: Experience with telemetry monitoring in an acute care setting.
- Required: Current Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card.
- Preferred: Nurse Manager Certificate.
