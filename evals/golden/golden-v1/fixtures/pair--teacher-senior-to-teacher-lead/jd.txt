Instructional Coach
Northfield Learning Network — Hybrid, Minneapolis, MN

Summary
Northfield Learning Network is a nonprofit school network working so every child reads, reasons and learns with confidence. The Instructional Coach works alongside teachers in a hybrid role based in Minneapolis, MN, supporting lesson planning, classroom practice and student progress across our schools. A current state teaching licence and the Instructional Leadership Endorsement are required for this position.

Duties
- Coach teachers on adapting work for different learners, including students who need extra support or extension.
- Support teachers in planning daily lessons that align with network curriculum and student needs.
- Help teachers prepare for parent conferences with clear evidence of student progress.
- Observe classrooms and give practical feedback on instruction and routines.
- Lead planning meetings with grade-level teams to share strategies and student work.

Licensure and qualifications
- Instructional Leadership Endorsement required.
- 12 or more years of experience in elementary education, including coaching or lead teaching.
- Working knowledge of Google Classroom and Nearpod.
- Small-group instruction and phonics instruction experience in the early grades.
- Formative assessment and reading intervention skills, with the ability to coach others in both.
