Instructional Coach
Northfield Learning Network — Hybrid, Minneapolis, MN

Summary
Northfield Learning Network is a nonprofit school network working so every child can read, reason and thrive. We are seeking an Instructional Coach to support teachers across our schools through classroom coaching, help with lesson planning and close use of student work. This hybrid role is based in Minneapolis, MN. A current State Teaching Licence is required.

Duties
- Coach teachers on adapting lessons and materials for different learners, including students who need extra support or extension
- Plan daily lessons with teachers and model instruction in their classrooms
- Support teachers in preparing for and leading parent conferences
- Review student work and assessment results with teachers to guide next steps
- Lead planning meetings with grade-level teams

Licensure and qualifications
- Current State Teaching Licence (required)
- 12 or more years of experience in elementary teaching or instructional coaching
- Skill in small-group instruction, phonics instruction and reading intervention
- Regular use of formative assessment to guide planning
- Working knowledge of Google Classroom and Nearpod
