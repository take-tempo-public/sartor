Senior Project Manager
Stonebridge Commercial Builders — On site, Phoenix, AZ

Position summary
Stonebridge Commercial Builders seeks a Senior Project Manager to lead commercial construction projects on site in Phoenix, AZ. The role oversees schedules, budgets, subcontractor coordination and safety planning from preconstruction through closeout. At minimum, candidates bring commercial project management experience, permit management and change order control. Preferred candidates also hold a construction safety certification.

Responsibilities
- Lead regular site safety walks with superintendents and subcontractors and track corrective actions to closure.
- Review submittals for compliance with drawings and specifications before release to the field.
- Price change orders, negotiate with subcontractors and present recommendations to the owner.
- Maintain project schedules and report progress at owner meetings.
- Coordinate permits and inspections with local authorities.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 8 or more years of commercial construction project management experience.
- Working knowledge of Oracle Primavera, permit management and subcontractor coordination.
- Demonstrated safety planning and contract administration on active job sites.
- Experience with schedule recovery on projects that have fallen behind.
- Construction Safety Manager Certificate required.
- Preferred: experience with Procore and Bluebeam Revu.
