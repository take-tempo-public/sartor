Senior Project Manager
Stonebridge Commercial Builders — On site, Phoenix, AZ

Position summary
Stonebridge Commercial Builders seeks a Senior Project Manager to lead commercial construction projects on site in Phoenix, AZ. The Senior Project Manager oversees budgets, schedules, subcontractor coordination and site safety from preconstruction through closeout. Minimum requirements include construction project management experience, scheduling software proficiency and knowledge of permit management. Preferred candidates hold a degree in construction management.

Responsibilities
- Lead regular site safety walks with superintendents and subcontractors, and track corrective actions to closure.
- Review submittals against contract documents and specifications before materials are released for fabrication.
- Price change orders, verify subcontractor quotes and present cost and schedule impacts to the owner.
- Maintain project budgets and schedules, and report status to company leadership.
- Coordinate permits and inspections with local agencies to keep work sequenced.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Minimum: 8 or more years of construction project management experience.
- Minimum: Proficiency in Oracle Primavera, including schedule recovery on active projects.
- Minimum: Experience with permit management, subcontractor coordination and safety planning.
- Minimum: Working knowledge of contract administration on commercial projects.
- Required certification: Construction Safety Manager Certificate.
- Preferred: Bachelor's degree in construction management or a related field.
