Program Manager
Armitage Utilities — Hybrid, Kansas City, MO

Position summary: Armitage Utilities, a regional utility, seeks a Program Manager to lead a portfolio of related projects on a hybrid basis from Kansas City, MO. The Program Manager will coordinate schedules, budgets, risks and vendors, and will report program progress to the steering committee. Candidates must meet the minimum qualifications listed below; a shorter list of preferred qualifications is also provided.

Responsibilities
- Maintain risk and issue logs for all projects in the program and drive timely resolution of open items.
- Track schedules and milestones across related projects and identify slippage early.
- Prepare and present regular progress reports to the steering committee.
- Escalate decisions, risks and dependencies to the steering committee with clear recommendations.
- Coordinate project managers and vendors to keep delivery aligned with approved scope and budget.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Minimum: 14 or more years of experience in project or program management.
- Minimum: Demonstrated status reporting and stakeholder communication with senior leadership.
- Minimum: Working knowledge of Microsoft Project, Confluence and Asana.
- Minimum: Proven vendor negotiation experience.
- Required certification: Agile Delivery Certificate.
- Preferred: Prior program management experience at a regional utility.
