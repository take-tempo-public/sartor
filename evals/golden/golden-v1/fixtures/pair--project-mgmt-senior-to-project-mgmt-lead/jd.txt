Program Manager
Armitage Utilities — Hybrid, Kansas City, MO

Position summary
Armitage Utilities, a regional utility, seeks a Program Manager to direct a portfolio of related projects from its Kansas City, MO office on a hybrid basis. The Program Manager will oversee schedules, risks and budgets, coordinate vendors and report progress to the steering committee.
Minimum: progressive project or program management experience, sound status reporting and vendor management.
Preferred: experience in the utility sector.

Responsibilities
- Maintain risk and issue logs for all program workstreams and drive timely resolution.
- Track schedules and milestones against approved baselines and address variances.
- Prepare and present regular progress reports to the steering committee.
- Manage change control for scope, schedule and budget adjustments.
- Coordinate vendors and internal teams to meet program commitments.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 14 or more years of project or program management experience.
- Demonstrated status reporting and stakeholder communication skills.
- Working knowledge of Microsoft Project, Confluence and Asana.
- Proven vendor negotiation experience.
- Required certification: Project Practitioner Certificate.
