Delivery Driver
Fairlane Parcel Delivery — On site, Columbus, OH

Position summary
Fairlane Parcel Delivery seeks a Delivery Driver to transport and deliver parcels to customers in Columbus, Ohio. The Delivery Driver inspects the assigned vehicle, secures each load, follows dispatch instructions and completes deliveries on schedule. At minimum, candidates must hold a Driver Safety Certificate, have prior delivery driving experience and know defensive driving and load documentation. A Commercial Driver Licence is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Conduct pre-trip vehicle inspections and report any defects before departure.
- Secure each load and check its contents against shipping documents.
- Deliver parcels to customers on time and in good condition.
- Follow assigned routes and coordinate schedule changes with dispatch.
- Obtain customer confirmation at each stop and record delivery status.
- Maintain accurate driver logs and complete required paperwork at the end of each shift.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 1 or more years of experience as a delivery or commercial driver.
- Working knowledge of dispatch software, bills of lading and customer signoff procedures.
- Demonstrated defensive driving practice and competence in trailer coupling.
- Training in hazmat handling and safe load securement.
- Required: a current Driver Safety Certificate.
- Preferred: a Commercial Driver Licence.
