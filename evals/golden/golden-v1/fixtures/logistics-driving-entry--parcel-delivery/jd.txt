Delivery Driver
Fairlane Parcel Delivery — On site, Columbus, OH

Position summary
Fairlane Parcel Delivery seeks a Delivery Driver to transport parcels safely and deliver them to customers on schedule from our Columbus, OH site. The Delivery Driver works with dispatch and follows company safety and documentation standards. At minimum, candidates must hold a Driver Safety Certificate and have prior delivery experience. A Commercial Driver Licence is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Conduct pre-trip vehicle inspections and report any defects before departure
- Secure each load and check it against shipping paperwork before leaving the site
- Deliver parcels to customers on time along assigned routes
- Obtain customer confirmation at the point of delivery and record any exceptions
- Communicate with dispatch about delays, route changes and delivery status
- Follow company safety procedures and traffic regulations at all times

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Minimum: 1 or more years of experience as a delivery driver
- Minimum: working knowledge of dispatch software, bills of lading and customer signoff procedures
- Minimum: skill in trailer coupling and defensive driving
- Minimum: training in hazmat handling
- Required certification: Driver Safety Certificate
- Preferred: Commercial Driver Licence
