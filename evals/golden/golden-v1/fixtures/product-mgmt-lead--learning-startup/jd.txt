Director of Product
Kestrel Learning — Remote (US)

Kestrel Learning builds education software, and we need a Director of Product to own where it goes next. You'll lead our product managers, set the roadmap with leadership, and keep us honest about what learners and teachers actually need. Expect real ownership, quick decisions and very little ceremony.

What you'll do
- Run regular metrics reviews and turn what the data shows into clear roadmap calls
- Coach and grow our product managers through feedback, pairing and hands-on support
- Own feature launches from first spec to release, working closely with engineering, design and marketing
- Set product priorities with leadership and explain the trade-offs plainly
- Talk with learners and teachers often so customer needs stay at the center of our work

What we're looking for
- 15 or more years of experience in product management
- Strong requirements writing and product analytics skills
- Hands-on pricing analysis and go-to-market planning for software products
- A track record of stakeholder alignment across leadership, engineering and sales
- Comfort planning and tracking delivery in Jira
