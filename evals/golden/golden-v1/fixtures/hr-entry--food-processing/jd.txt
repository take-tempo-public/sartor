HR Coordinator
Orchard Foods Corporation — On site, Fresno, CA

Position summary
Orchard Foods Corporation seeks an HR Coordinator to support human resources operations at its food-processing facility in Fresno, California. The coordinator will serve as a point of contact for employees and managers on routine HR matters and will work on site with the plant leadership team.
Minimum: prior HR support experience, working knowledge of HR information systems, and clear written communication.
Preferred: experience in a manufacturing or food-processing setting.

Responsibilities
- Answer employee questions about benefits enrollment, eligibility and coverage changes
- Maintain accurate and confidential HR records in the HR information system
- Communicate policy updates to employees and supervisors across production shifts
- Update employee files to reflect changes in status, pay and job assignments
- Assist with audits of HR records and follow up on missing documentation

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 2 or more years of experience in an HR coordinator or HR support role
- Working experience with payroll coordination and applicant tracking
- Demonstrated ability to manage new hire onboarding and performance review cycles
- Experience supporting employee relations matters and conducting exit interviews
- Preferred: HR Practitioner Certificate
