Senior Product Designer
Riverside Care Alliance — Remote (US)

Summary
Riverside Care Alliance is a telehealth nonprofit working to bring reliable care to patients wherever they live. We are hiring a Senior Product Designer to shape the patient and clinician experience across our virtual care services. This is a remote role open to candidates in the US. No clinical licence or professional certification is required for this position.

Duties
- Mentor designers through regular feedback, pairing and design critique
- Plan and run usability sessions with patients, caregivers and clinicians
- Identify accessibility issues and work with engineering to ship fixes
- Turn research findings into flows, wireframes and prototypes
- Present design decisions to clinical and product stakeholders

Licensure and qualifications
- 8 or more years of experience in product or UX design
- Interaction design and wireframing skills shown in a portfolio of shipped work
- Information architecture experience with complex, multi-step services
- Accessibility review experience, including auditing screens and tracking fixes
- Prototyping skills and working knowledge of HTML and CSS
