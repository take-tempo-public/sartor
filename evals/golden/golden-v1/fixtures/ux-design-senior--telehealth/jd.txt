Senior Product Designer
Riverside Care Alliance — Remote (US)

Summary
Riverside Care Alliance is a telehealth nonprofit working to make quality care reachable from home. We are seeking a Senior Product Designer to shape patient and clinician experiences across our remote care services. This role does not require a clinical licence or certification. The position is remote within the US.

Duties
- Mentor designers through regular feedback, pairing and design critique.
- Plan and run usability sessions with patients, caregivers and clinicians.
- Identify accessibility issues and partner with engineering to ship fixes.
- Translate research findings into clear flows, wireframes and prototypes.
- Present design direction to stakeholders and incorporate their feedback.

Licensure and qualifications
- 8 or more years of experience in product or UX design.
- Strong skills in Interaction design and Information architecture.
- Proficiency in Wireframing and Prototyping for web and mobile experiences.
- Experience with Accessibility review for digital products.
- Working knowledge of HTML and CSS to collaborate with engineers.
