Carpentry Foreman
Brandt Commercial Construction — On site, Denver, CO

Position summary
Brandt Commercial Construction, a commercial general contractor, seeks a Carpentry Foreman to lead carpentry crews on site in Denver, CO. The Carpentry Foreman directs daily work, coordinates with site supervision and upholds quality and safety standards. Minimum qualifications include journeyman-level carpentry experience and demonstrated crew leadership. Preferred qualifications include formal supervisory training.

Responsibilities
- Build, set and brace forms for concrete placement
- Read plans and specifications and take accurate field measurements
- Plan daily job sequences and assign tasks to carpentry crews
- Order lumber and materials and track deliveries against the schedule
- Inspect completed work for accuracy and conformance to plans

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 12 or more years of carpentry experience, including crew leadership
- Proficiency in framing, stair building and cabinet installation
- Demonstrated scaffold erection and power tool use
- Working knowledge of jobsite safety practices
- Required certification: Construction Safety Card
- Preferred: completed carpentry apprenticeship
