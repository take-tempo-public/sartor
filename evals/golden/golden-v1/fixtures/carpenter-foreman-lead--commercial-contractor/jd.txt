Carpentry Foreman
Brandt Commercial Construction — On site, Denver, CO

Position summary
Brandt Commercial Construction seeks a Carpentry Foreman to lead carpentry crews on commercial projects in Denver. The Carpentry Foreman directs daily field work, coordinates with project leadership, and maintains schedule, quality and safety standards on site. Minimum requirements include framing, concrete forming and blueprint reading experience. Preferred candidates also bring experience with material takeoffs.

Responsibilities
- Build, set and strip forms for concrete footings, walls and slabs.
- Read plans and drawings, take field measurements, and lay out work accurately.
- Plan daily and weekly carpentry tasks and assign work to crew members.
- Prepare material takeoffs and order lumber and hardware to meet the schedule.
- Inspect completed work for accuracy and conformance to drawings.
- Enforce jobsite safety practices across the carpentry crew.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 12 or more years of carpentry experience.
- Demonstrated skill in framing, stair building and cabinet installation.
- Proficiency in scaffold erection and power tool use.
- Working knowledge of jobsite safety practices and regulations.
- Required certification: Crew Supervisor Certificate.
- Preferred: experience leading carpentry crews on commercial projects.
