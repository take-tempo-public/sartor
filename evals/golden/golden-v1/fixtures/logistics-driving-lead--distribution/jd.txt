Transportation Supervisor
Beacon Distribution Group — On site, Kansas City, MO

Beacon Distribution Group seeks a Transportation Supervisor to lead on-site driver operations in Kansas City, MO. The supervisor directs daily load movement, works closely with dispatch and keeps the fleet compliant with safety and logging requirements. Minimum requirements include extensive commercial driving experience and sound hazmat knowledge. Preferred candidates also hold dispatch certification.

Responsibilities
- Secure and check inbound and outbound loads before release.
- Coordinate daily with dispatch on driver assignments and delivery timing.
- Keep driver logs accurate and audit hours-of-service records.
- Oversee pre-trip inspections and follow up on reported vehicle defects.
- Train and coach drivers on safe operating practices.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 15 or more years of commercial driving or transportation experience.
- Demonstrated skill in hours-of-service logging, pre-trip inspections and hazmat handling.
- Experience with route planning and delivery scheduling.
- Working knowledge of dispatch software.
- Required certification: Dispatch Coordinator Certificate.
- Preferred: supervisory experience leading a driver team.
