Transportation Supervisor
Beacon Distribution Group — On site, Kansas City, MO

Position summary
Beacon Distribution Group seeks a Transportation Supervisor to oversee daily driver and fleet operations at its Kansas City distribution site. The Transportation Supervisor directs drivers, works closely with dispatch, and ensures that loads move safely, on schedule and in compliance with applicable regulations. Minimum requirements include commercial driving experience and prior supervisory responsibility. Experience with hazardous materials freight is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Supervise the securing and checking of loads before trucks leave the facility.
- Coordinate with dispatch on driver assignments, departure times and route changes.
- Review and maintain driver logs to confirm accurate hours-of-service records.
- Confirm that drivers complete vehicle inspections and report defects promptly.
- Train and coach drivers on safe operating practices and company procedures.
- Resolve delivery issues with dispatch and customers as they arise.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Minimum: 15 or more years of commercial driving and transportation experience.
- Minimum: Demonstrated hours-of-service logging, route planning and pre-trip inspections.
- Minimum: Experience with hazmat handling and delivery scheduling.
- Minimum: Working knowledge of dispatch software.
- Required certification: Dispatch Coordinator Certificate.
- Preferred: Experience with cargo documentation and fuel and mileage logs.
