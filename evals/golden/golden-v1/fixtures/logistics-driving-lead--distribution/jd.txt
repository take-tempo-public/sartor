Transportation Supervisor
Beacon Distribution Group — On site, Kansas City, MO

Position summary
Beacon Distribution Group seeks a Transportation Supervisor to oversee daily driver and fleet operations at its Kansas City site. The supervisor directs load security, dispatch coordination and driver log compliance. Minimum requirements: commercial driving experience and a valid safety certification. Preferred: hazmat experience and prior supervisory responsibility.

Responsibilities
- Secure and check outbound loads before they are released from the site
- Coordinate with dispatch on driver assignments and delivery schedules
- Maintain accurate driver logs and review hours-of-service records
- Oversee pre-trip inspections and the reporting of vehicle defects
- Train and support drivers on safe and compliant operations

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 15 or more years of commercial transportation experience
- Demonstrated skill in hours-of-service logging, route planning and delivery scheduling
- Working knowledge of hazmat handling and pre-trip inspections
- Experience with dispatch software
- Required certification: Driver Safety Certificate
