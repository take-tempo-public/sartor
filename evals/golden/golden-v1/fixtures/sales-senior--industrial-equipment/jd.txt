Senior Account Executive
Ellison Industrial Equipment — Hybrid, Cincinnati, OH

Position summary
Ellison Industrial Equipment seeks a Senior Account Executive to manage the full sales cycle for its industrial equipment portfolio. The role covers qualifying new opportunities, negotiating contracts and supporting long-term customer relationships. This is a hybrid position based in Cincinnati, OH.
Minimum requirements:
- Experience in business-to-business sales
- A record of closing new accounts
- Clear written and verbal communication
Preferred:
- Experience selling industrial equipment
- Familiarity with manufacturing buyers

Responsibilities
- Qualify inbound and outbound leads against agreed criteria before advancing them in the pipeline
- Prepare accurate quotes and manage contracts from first draft through signature
- Negotiate pricing and terms with buyers in line with company guidelines
- Coordinate a structured handoff of closed accounts to the customer success team
- Maintain current records of opportunities and forecasts in the CRM
- Work with internal teams to resolve customer questions during the sales cycle

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 7 or more years of experience in business-to-business sales
- Proposal writing skills for technical and commercial offers
- Discovery calls that uncover customer needs and buying criteria
- Contract negotiation experience with pricing and terms
- Working knowledge of HubSpot or Salesforce
- Preferred: experience prospecting with Sales Navigator
