Senior Data Scientist
Meridian Mutual — Hybrid, Chicago, IL

Position summary
Meridian Mutual seeks a Senior Data Scientist to develop, validate and maintain predictive models that support underwriting, pricing and claims decisions. The role works on a hybrid schedule from Chicago and partners with actuarial, product and technology teams.
Minimum: a degree in a quantitative field and substantial experience building production models.
Preferred: experience in the insurance industry.

Responsibilities
- Lead validation of predictive models, including performance testing, stability checks and documentation for review.
- Direct feature selection for underwriting, pricing and claims models, balancing accuracy with interpretability.
- Mentor analysts and data scientists through code review, modeling guidance and structured feedback.
- Partner with actuarial and business teams to define modeling requirements and success measures.
- Present findings and model recommendations to leadership in clear, actionable terms.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 9 or more years of experience in data science, statistical modeling or a related field.
- Advanced SQL skills and hands-on experience with Snowflake.
- Proficiency in Python and Jupyter for analysis and model development.
- Experience orchestrating data and model pipelines with Airflow.
- Experience building Tableau dashboards for business audiences.
- Preferred: background in insurance analytics.
