HR Manager
Nimbus Cloud Software — Remote (US)

About the role
We're a small software team growing fast, and we need an HR Manager who can build the people side with us. You'll be the go-to person for managers and employees alike, keeping our policies clear, our hiring smooth and our people supported. If you like rolling up your sleeves and fixing things before they turn into problems, we'd love to talk.

What you'll do
- Advise managers on employee relations questions and coach them through difficult conversations
- Listen to employee concerns, investigate fairly and follow through on resolutions
- Keep our policies current and roll out policy updates in plain language
- Partner with team leads on hiring and onboarding so new people get up to speed quickly
- Spot patterns in employee feedback and recommend practical fixes

What we're looking for
- 13 or more years of experience in human resources
- Solid compliance reporting and policy writing skills
- Experience managing job postings, interview scheduling and new hire onboarding
- Hands-on experience with ADP Workforce Now
- HR Practitioner Certificate (required)
