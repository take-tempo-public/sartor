HR Manager
Nimbus Cloud Software — Remote (US)

About the role
We're Nimbus Cloud Software, and we're growing fast. We need an HR Manager who can keep our people practices simple, fair and compliant while we scale. You'll work remotely across the US, be the go-to person for managers and employees, and build the processes we don't have yet. If you like rolling up your sleeves and getting things done, you'll fit right in.

What you'll do
- Advise managers on employee relations, from tricky conversations to formal investigations
- Be the first stop for employee concerns and see each one through to resolution
- Write and roll out policy updates as we grow, and explain them in plain language
- Coach managers on documentation and consistent decisions
- Spot patterns in employee concerns and suggest fixes before they become bigger problems

What we're looking for
- 13 or more years of HR experience
- Senior HR Practitioner Certificate (required)
- Solid compliance reporting and policy writing skills
- Hands-on experience with job postings, interview scheduling and new hire onboarding
- Day-to-day experience with ADP Workforce Now
