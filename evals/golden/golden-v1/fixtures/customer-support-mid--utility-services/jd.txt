Customer Support Specialist
Corvallis Energy Services — Hybrid, Phoenix, AZ

Position summary
Corvallis Energy Services seeks a Customer Support Specialist to assist utility customers with account, billing and service questions in a hybrid role based in Phoenix, AZ. The specialist will resolve inquiries accurately, keep records complete and help maintain customer-facing support content. Minimum qualifications include phone support experience, account verification practice and clear written communication. Preferred qualifications include Salesforce experience.

Responsibilities
- Update help articles and support content so customers receive current, accurate guidance on utility accounts and billing.
- Track customer satisfaction scores and report trends to support leadership.
- Document each ticket with clear notes on the request, actions taken and resolution.
- Respond to customer inquiries by phone and written channels in a courteous, professional manner.
- Escalate unresolved or technical issues to the appropriate internal teams with complete supporting detail.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 5 or more years of customer support experience.
- Demonstrated experience with phone support in a customer-facing environment.
- Working knowledge of account verification procedures and customer data protection.
- Experience administering satisfaction surveys and acting on the results.
- Knowledge base writing experience, including drafting and maintaining help articles.
- Preferred: experience conducting onboarding calls and working in Salesforce.
