Apprentice Carpenter
Pennington Custom Homes — On site, Asheville, NC

About us and the job
Pennington Custom Homes is an owner-run builder of custom homes in Asheville, NC. We build every house on site, and the owner is out on the job most days. We are looking for an apprentice carpenter who wants to learn the trade by doing real work alongside experienced carpenters, from the first concrete forms to the last piece of trim.

What the job involves
- Keep the site safe and tidy, follow safety rules and report hazards right away
- Install doors and windows so they sit level, plumb and sealed
- Cut and install interior and exterior trim with clean, tight joints
- Build and brace forms for concrete footings, walls and slabs
- Carry materials, set up tools and help the crew wherever needed

What you need
- 1 or more years of experience in carpentry or construction
- Skills in scaffold erection, jobsite safety and blueprint reading
- Experience with finish trim, power tool use and subfloor installation
- A current Construction Safety Card (required)
- An Apprenticeship Completion Certificate (preferred)
