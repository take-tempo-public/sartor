HVAC Technician
Aspen Ridge Mechanical — On site, Salt Lake City, UT

About us and the job
Aspen Ridge Mechanical is an owner-run heating and cooling company serving homes in and around Salt Lake City. We are looking for an HVAC technician to join our field crew. The work is on site in customers' homes, servicing, repairing and installing furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps. We keep our crew small, treat customers fairly and stand behind our work.

What the job involves
- Visit homes to diagnose heating and cooling faults and carry out repairs
- Estimate jobs for customers and explain costs and options in plain terms
- Test systems for refrigerant leaks and repair them to code
- Service furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps on maintenance visits
- Keep clear work orders and records for each job

What you need
- 4 or more years of experience as an HVAC technician
- A current HVAC Technician Licence (required)
- Hands-on skill in heat pump service, system diagnostics and leak testing
- Experience with building automation and thermostat installation
- Brazing and soldering on copper line sets and fittings
- A Controls Technician Certificate is preferred
