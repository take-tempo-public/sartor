Project Coordinator
Garrick Engineering Group — On site, Denver, CO

Position summary
Garrick Engineering Group seeks a Project Coordinator to support engineering project teams on site at its Denver office. The coordinator will maintain project schedules, coordinate vendors and assist with project close-out. Minimum qualifications include experience with scheduling tools, status reporting and budget tracking. Preferred qualifications include the Project Practitioner Certificate.

Responsibilities
- Track project schedules and milestones, and alert project managers to slipping tasks.
- Coordinate with vendors on deliverables, timelines and documentation.
- Prepare regular status updates for project teams and clients.
- Maintain schedule records and supporting files in shared project tools.
- Lead project close-out activities, including final documentation and handover checklists.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 2 or more years of experience in project coordination or a related role.
- Working knowledge of Smartsheet, Jira and Gantt scheduling.
- Experience facilitating or supporting Agile ceremonies.
- Demonstrated skill in status reporting and budget tracking.
- Preferred: Project Practitioner Certificate.
