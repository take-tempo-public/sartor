Junior Security Analyst
Weston Credit Union Services — Hybrid, Richmond, VA

Position summary
Weston Credit Union Services, a regional credit union, seeks a Junior Security Analyst to help protect member data and internal systems. The analyst will work in a hybrid arrangement from Richmond, VA, under the direction of senior security staff. Minimum requirements include working knowledge of incident response, log analysis and Nessus. A Security Analyst Certificate is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Triage security alerts, determine their severity and escalate confirmed incidents to senior analysts.
- Track remediation of identified vulnerabilities and follow up with system owners until fixes are verified.
- Perform periodic access reviews and report inappropriate or outdated permissions for correction.
- Review firewall rules and system logs for activity that does not match established policy.
- Document findings, actions taken and open items in the team's tracking system.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 1 or more years of experience in a security analyst or related role.
- Working knowledge of incident response procedures and log analysis.
- Experience reviewing and maintaining firewall rules.
- Hands-on use of Nessus for vulnerability assessment.
- Familiarity with Python and Linux in an operational setting.
- Preferred: Security Analyst Certificate.
