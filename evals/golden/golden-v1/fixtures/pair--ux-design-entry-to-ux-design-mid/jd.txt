UX Designer
Westbrook Insurance Group — Hybrid, Des Moines, IA

Position summary
Westbrook Insurance Group seeks a UX Designer to shape digital experiences for policyholders, agents and claims staff. The role sits within a hybrid team based in Des Moines, IA, and partners closely with product and engineering. Minimum: professional UX design experience, a portfolio of shipped work, and working knowledge of accessibility standards. Preferred: experience with insurance or financial services products.

Responsibilities
- Identify accessibility issues across digital products and work with engineering to deliver fixes.
- Synthesize user research into clear findings and design recommendations.
- Plan and run usability sessions with policyholders, agents and internal users.
- Translate session results into revised designs and share them with stakeholders.
- Contribute to shared design standards and documentation.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 5 or more years of experience in UX or product design.
- Proficiency in Figma and Adobe Illustrator.
- Wireframing and prototyping skills for web and mobile flows.
- A strong grounding in interaction design principles.
- Comfort giving and receiving feedback through design critique.
- Preferred: experience designing for insurance or financial services.
