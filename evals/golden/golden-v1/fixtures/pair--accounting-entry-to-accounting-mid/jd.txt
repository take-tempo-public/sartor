Accountant
Kensington Manufacturing Group — On site, Milwaukee, WI

Position summary
Kensington Manufacturing Group seeks an Accountant to join its finance team on site in Milwaukee, WI. The Accountant will review invoices, prepare tax schedules and support audit preparation for manufacturing operations. At a minimum, candidates must bring hands-on general ledger experience and strong spreadsheet skills. A professional accounting licence is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Review vendor invoices for accuracy, approval and correct account coding before payment.
- Prepare sales and use tax schedules and supporting reconciliations for timely filing.
- Assemble schedules, documentation and account analyses for internal and external audits.
- Record journal entries and reconcile general ledger accounts during the month-end close.
- Track fixed assets and maintain depreciation records for plant equipment.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 5 or more years of accounting experience.
- Demonstrated skill in budget variance analysis, fixed asset tracking and accounts receivable.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and SAP.
- Experience preparing and posting journal entries.
- Required certification: Bookkeeping Certificate.
- Preferred licence: Certified Public Accountant Licence.
