Medical Assistant
Cedar Ridge Community Health — On site, Tacoma, WA

Summary
Cedar Ridge Community Health provides accessible, compassionate primary care to every member of our community, regardless of ability to pay. We are seeking an on-site Medical Assistant in Tacoma, WA to support our providers and patients through daily clinical and front-office work. A current Phlebotomy Certificate is required for this position.

Duties
- Take and record vital signs for patients before each provider visit.
- Stock and restock exam rooms with supplies so rooms are ready throughout the day.
- Relay physician instructions to patients clearly and confirm they understand next steps.
- Room patients and prepare them for examinations and procedures.
- Update patient records accurately and keep the care team informed of changes.

Licensure and qualifications
- Phlebotomy Certificate required.
- 4 or more years of experience as a medical assistant in a clinical setting.
- Experience with specimen handling and patient intake.
- Working knowledge of appointment scheduling and insurance verification.
- Familiarity with medical coding and prior authorizations.
