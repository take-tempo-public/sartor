Medical Assistant
Cedar Ridge Community Health — On site, Tacoma, WA

Cedar Ridge Community Health provides accessible primary care to every member of our community, regardless of ability to pay. We are seeking a Medical Assistant to join our on-site care team in Tacoma, WA. The Medical Assistant supports providers by preparing patients for visits, assisting with clinical procedures and keeping clinic flow organized. Applicants must hold the Certified Medical Assistant Credential.

Duties
- Take and record vital signs for each patient before the provider visit
- Room patients and document the reason for the visit in the health record
- Stock exam rooms with supplies and keep them clean and ready between appointments
- Relay physician instructions to patients clearly and confirm understanding before they leave
- Assist providers with exams and routine clinical procedures as directed
- Coordinate follow-up visits and referrals with the front desk team

Licensure and qualifications
- Certified Medical Assistant Credential required
- 4 or more years of experience as a medical assistant in an outpatient or community health setting
- Experience with patient intake, specimen handling and appointment scheduling
- Working knowledge of insurance verification and prior authorizations
- Familiarity with medical coding for outpatient visits
