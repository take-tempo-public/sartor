Senior Truck Driver
Stillwater Produce Haulers — On site, Fresno, CA

Stillwater Produce Haulers is a family-owned business that moves fresh produce by truck. We are looking for a Senior Truck Driver to join our team. This is an on-site job in Fresno, CA. You will work out of our yard alongside our drivers and dispatch staff, and you will help keep our loads moving safely and on schedule.

What the job involves
- Train new drivers on safe driving, inspections and our daily routines
- Deliver produce to customers on time and in good condition
- Coordinate with dispatch on pickups, drop-offs and schedule changes
- Check loads and paperwork before each run
- Report vehicle problems so trucks are repaired before they go back on the road

What you need
- 9 or more years of experience as a truck driver
- Hands-on skill in pre-trip inspections and vehicle maintenance checks
- Solid habits in defensive driving and route planning
- Accurate cargo documentation and fuel and mileage logs
- A current Hazardous Materials Endorsement (required)
- A Dispatch Coordinator Certificate (preferred)
