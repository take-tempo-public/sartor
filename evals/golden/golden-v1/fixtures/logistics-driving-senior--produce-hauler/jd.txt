Senior Truck Driver
Stillwater Produce Haulers — On site, Fresno, CA

About us and the job
Stillwater Produce Haulers is a family-owned business that moves fresh produce for growers and buyers across the region. The owners run the yard themselves and know every driver by name. We are looking for a Senior Truck Driver to join us on site in Fresno, CA. You will haul produce safely, keep loads fresh and on schedule, and help our newer drivers learn the job the right way.

What the job involves
- Haul fresh produce to customers on time and in good condition
- Train new drivers on safe driving, loading and paperwork
- Coordinate with dispatch on pickups, delivery windows and route changes
- Check loads and trailers before each run and report any problems
- Keep delivery paperwork accurate and get customer signoff at each stop
- Help the team handle difficult or time-sensitive deliveries

What you need
- 9 or more years of experience driving trucks
- Pre-trip inspections and vehicle maintenance checks done carefully before every run
- Cargo documentation and fuel and mileage logs kept accurate and up to date
- Defensive driving and route planning skills for produce deliveries
- Hazardous Materials Endorsement is required
- Dispatch Coordinator Certificate is preferred
