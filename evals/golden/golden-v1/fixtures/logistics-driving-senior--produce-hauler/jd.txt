Senior Truck Driver
Stillwater Produce Haulers — On site, Fresno, CA

About us and the job
Stillwater Produce Haulers is a family-owned business that moves fresh produce from local farms to stores and markets. We are looking for a Senior Truck Driver to work on site from our yard in Fresno, CA. You will drive our routes, look after our trucks and help our newer drivers learn the job. We treat our drivers like family and expect careful, steady work in return.

What the job involves
- Train new drivers on our trucks, routes and safety habits
- Deliver fresh produce to customers on time and in good condition
- Coordinate with dispatch on pickups, delivery windows and schedule changes
- Check loads, paperwork and trucks before every trip
- Report vehicle problems so they are fixed before the next run
- Keep in touch with customers and the office when delays come up

What you need
- 9 or more years of experience driving trucks
- Solid habits in pre-trip inspections and vehicle maintenance checks
- Careful cargo documentation and accurate fuel and mileage logs
- Skill in defensive driving and route planning
- Required: Driver Safety Certificate
- Preferred: Dispatch Coordinator Certificate
