Associate Product Manager
Skylark Software — Remote (US)

Skylark builds workflow software for teams who are tired of chasing status updates across a dozen tools. As our Associate Product Manager, you'll own a clear slice of the product, talk with customers, dig into the data and ship alongside engineering and design. You'll have a senior PM in your corner, plenty of room to learn and real ownership from your first week.

What you'll do
- Keep engineering, design, support and sales in sync on what's shipping and why
- Run regular metrics reviews and turn what you find into clear next steps
- Set up and lead customer interviews to understand how teams actually get work done
- Write crisp specs and user stories with your senior PM reviewing
- Track feature launches from kickoff to follow-up and share what we learned
- Help shape the backlog based on customer evidence and usage data

What we're looking for
- 1 or more years of experience in product management, product operations or a closely related role
- Comfort with customer discovery and turning interview notes into product decisions
- Pricing analysis and go-to-market planning experience, even on a small scope
- Hands-on use of Tableau for dashboards and Figma for reviewing or sketching designs
- Clear written documentation habits in Confluence or a similar tool
