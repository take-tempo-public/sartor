Engineering Manager
Tidewater Freight Labs — Remote (US)

We're Tidewater Freight Labs, and we help shippers see where their freight is right now. We need an Engineering Manager to lead our backend team, keep our tracking APIs fast and reliable, and help decide what we build next. You'll still read code, review designs and get your hands dirty when it counts. The role is fully remote within the US.

What you'll do
- Lead API design and versioning so partners can integrate without surprises
- Automate our deployment pipeline from commit to production
- Track down slow queries and tune them before customers notice
- Run code reviews and mentor the engineers on your team
- Own the backend roadmap together with product and operations

What we're looking for
- 13 or more years of backend software experience, including time leading a team
- Hands-on work building Spring Boot services and shipping them with Docker
- Experience setting up and maintaining CI pipelines in Jenkins
- Comfort running PostgreSQL and Elasticsearch in production
- Solid Linux skills for debugging live systems
