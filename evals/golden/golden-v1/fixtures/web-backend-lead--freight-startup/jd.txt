Engineering Manager

We're looking for an Engineering Manager to lead our backend team, keep our APIs solid, and help us ship faster without breaking things. What we're looking for

- Hands-on work with Spring Boot services and PostgreSQL databases
- Comfort running Docker containers on Linux in production
