Project Manager
Tandem Software — Hybrid, Boston, MA

About the role
Tandem Software is growing, and so is the number of projects we run. We need a Project Manager who keeps work moving with clear plans, honest status updates and no surprises. You'll work with engineering, product and outside vendors to get projects across the line and closed out properly. This is a hybrid role based in Boston.

What you'll do
- Own project close-out: confirm deliverables, collect sign-offs and capture lessons learned
- Run the change request process, from intake and impact review through to approval
- Keep risk and issue logs current, with a clear owner and next step for every item
- Spot blockers early, escalate them and help teams work through them
- Share regular status updates with stakeholders across the company

What we're looking for
- 3 or more years of experience as a project manager or in a similar role
- Hands-on experience with Jira and Asana
- Solid resource planning and vendor negotiation skills
- Clear stakeholder communication and disciplined change control
- Required: Project Practitioner Certificate
- Preferred: Agile Delivery Certificate
