Account Executive
Pivotal Workflow — Remote (US)

Pivotal Workflow builds workflow software that helps teams stop chasing tasks and start finishing them. We're looking for an Account Executive who likes owning deals from first call to signature. You'll work remotely across the US, talk to a lot of operations leaders, and help shape how a growing sales team works. If you enjoy building pipeline and closing without a heavy playbook, we'd like to meet you.

What you'll do
- Prospect new accounts through outbound outreach, referrals and inbound interest
- Qualify leads quickly so time goes to buyers with real need, budget and timing
- Build quotes and send contracts, then work through terms with buyers until signature
- Run demos that show prospects how Pivotal Workflow fits their day-to-day process
- Keep deal notes and next steps current so the team always knows where things stand

What we're looking for
- 4 or more years of experience in business-to-business sales
- A track record of quota attainment you can walk us through
- Hands-on renewal management and comfort expanding existing accounts
- Daily use of Salesforce and disciplined pipeline management
- Confident product demonstrations and well-structured discovery calls
