Design Manager
Parable Learning — Hybrid, Denver, CO

About the role
Parable Learning builds online courses people actually finish, and we need a Design Manager to help us get there. You'll lead a small, scrappy design team, keep our product experience clear and accessible, and work side by side with engineering and content folks. This role is hybrid, based in Denver, CO. If you like shipping, coaching and solving messy problems for learners, let's talk.

What you'll do
- Lead and mentor our designers through regular feedback, pairing and career conversations
- Own wireframes for new learning flows, from rough sketches to clear, reviewable layouts
- Run smooth handoff to engineering with specs, annotations and quick follow-up when questions come up
- Set up and run design critiques so work gets sharper before it ships
- Partner with product and content leads to decide what the team builds next
- Keep our patterns consistent and usable across the whole product

What we're looking for
- 14 or more years of experience in product or UX design, including time leading designers
- Comfort running design critique sessions and giving direct, useful feedback
- Hands-on experience with accessibility review and fixing what it turns up
- Strong skills in Figma and Adobe Illustrator
- Experience running remote workshops in Miro
- Working knowledge of HTML and CSS so you can talk shop with engineers
