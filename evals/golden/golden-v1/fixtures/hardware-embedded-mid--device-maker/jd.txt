Hardware Engineer
Copperline Devices — On site, Minneapolis, MN

About us and the job
Copperline Devices is a small, owner-run company in Minneapolis that designs and builds medical devices. We are looking for a Hardware Engineer to help design, test and improve the boards inside our products. This is an on-site job. You will work at our bench alongside the people who build and support each device, and your work will go straight into products used by clinics and patients.

What the job involves
- Plan, write and release firmware updates for the boards in our devices
- Write validation test plans and run them before each design goes to production
- Choose components with our suppliers, weighing cost, availability and quality
- Debug prototype boards on the bench and track faults to their cause
- Keep design records clear so others in the team can follow your work

What you need
- 5 or more years of experience as a hardware engineer
- Hands-on skill in PCB layout for mixed-signal boards
- Working knowledge of Verilog and SPICE simulation
- Comfort writing Python scripts and working on Linux
- Experience using MATLAB for analysis of test data
