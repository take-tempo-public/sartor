Sales Manager
Trailhead Packaging — On site, Green Bay, WI

Position summary
Trailhead Packaging seeks a Sales Manager to lead its sales team on site in Green Bay, WI. The Sales Manager will direct a team of account executives, manage the sales pipeline and be accountable for revenue results across the customer base.
Minimum: business-to-business sales management experience and a record of meeting sales targets.
Preferred: experience selling packaging products.

Responsibilities
- Lead regular pipeline reviews with account executives and maintain an accurate sales forecast.
- Drive renewals and upsells across existing packaging accounts.
- Coach account executives on discovery, proposals and negotiation.
- Set territory and quota plans for the team and track progress against them.
- Report sales results and risks to company leadership.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 12 or more years of business-to-business sales experience, including team leadership.
- Strong proposal writing and cold outreach skills.
- Demonstrated quota attainment and territory planning experience.
- Skill in running discovery calls with technical and procurement buyers.
- Preferred: working knowledge of HubSpot.
