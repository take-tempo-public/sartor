Clinical Assistant
Maplewood Family Practice — On site, Dayton, OH

Maplewood Family Practice is a private, owner-run family practice in Dayton, Ohio. We care for patients of all ages, and many of them have been with us for years. We are looking for a clinical assistant to join our small team on site. You will help our providers keep the day running smoothly and make sure every patient feels looked after.

What the job involves
- Room patients and prepare them for the provider
- Schedule follow-up visits before patients leave and confirm them by phone or message
- Complete insurance forms accurately and send them on time
- Keep exam rooms clean, tidy and stocked
- Update patient charts after each visit

What you need
- 2 or more years of experience as a clinical or medical assistant
- Skill in patient intake, vital signs, EKG testing and injections
- Experience keeping medical records accurate and handling prior authorizations
- Certified Medical Assistant Credential (required)
- Phlebotomy Certificate (preferred)
