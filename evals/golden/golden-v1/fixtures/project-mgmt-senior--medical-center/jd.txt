Senior Project Manager
Lakeshore Medical Center — On site, Cleveland, OH

Summary
Lakeshore Medical Center exists to provide safe, compassionate care to every patient and family in our community. The Senior Project Manager leads projects that support clinical and operational teams, working on site in Cleveland, OH. The role coordinates scope, schedule and budget with hospital leaders and staff. A Project Practitioner Certificate is required.

Duties
- Review, log and route change requests, assessing their impact on scope, schedule and budget.
- Maintain risk and issue logs and assign owners for each follow-up action.
- Escalate high-impact risks and issues to department leaders and the project sponsor.
- Lead project close-out, including handover to operational teams and lessons learned.
- Archive final project documentation in line with hospital records policy.

Licensure and qualifications
- Project Practitioner Certificate required.
- 9 or more years of project management experience.
- Working knowledge of budget tracking and risk registers.
- Experience running Agile ceremonies and documenting work in Confluence and Asana.
- Clear stakeholder communication with clinical, administrative and vendor teams.
