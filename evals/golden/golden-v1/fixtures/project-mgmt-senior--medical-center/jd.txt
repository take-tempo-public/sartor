Senior Project Manager
Lakeshore Medical Center — On site, Cleveland, OH

Summary
Lakeshore Medical Center exists to provide safe, compassionate care to every patient and family in our community. We are seeking a Senior Project Manager to lead projects that support our clinical and operational teams, keeping work on schedule, within budget and aligned with patient care priorities. The Agile Delivery Certificate is required for this position.

Duties
- Review, log and route change requests, assessing their effect on scope, schedule and budget before approval.
- Maintain risk and issue logs for each project and assign owners and follow-up dates.
- Escalate high-priority risks and issues to department leaders and the steering group.
- Lead project close-out, including final reviews, document archiving and handover to operational teams.
- Capture lessons learned at close-out and share them with project staff across the medical center.

Licensure and qualifications
- Agile Delivery Certificate required.
- 9 or more years of experience in project management.
- Working knowledge of budget tracking and risk registers.
- Experience running Agile ceremonies for cross-functional teams.
- Proficiency with Confluence and Asana for documentation and task management.
- Clear stakeholder communication with clinical, administrative and technical staff.
