HVAC Technician
Aspen Ridge Mechanical — On site, Salt Lake City, UT

Aspen Ridge Mechanical is an owner-run residential heating and cooling company in Salt Lake City. We are hiring an HVAC Technician to work on site in customers' homes, servicing, repairing and installing furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps. You will deal directly with homeowners and work alongside our small team, and we expect careful, clean work done to code.

What the job involves
- Visit customers' homes to estimate jobs and explain the work and cost in plain terms
- Diagnose heating and cooling faults on furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps
- Test systems for refrigerant leaks and repair them to code
- Install and service equipment, then check that it runs as it should
- Keep clear records of each visit and the work done

What you need
- 4 or more years of experience as an HVAC technician
- A current HVAC Technician Licence is required; a Controls Technician Certificate is preferred
- Hands-on skill in heat pump service, system diagnostics and leak testing
- Experience with building automation and thermostat installation
- Brazing and soldering on copper lines and fittings
