HVAC Technician
Aspen Ridge Mechanical — On site, Salt Lake City, UT

Aspen Ridge Mechanical is an owner-run residential heating and cooling company in Salt Lake City. We look after homes in the area, and our technicians work on site in customers' houses every day. We are hiring an HVAC Technician who shows up on time, does careful work and explains it to homeowners in plain words.

What the job involves
- Visit homes to inspect heating and cooling systems and estimate jobs for customers
- Diagnose heating and cooling faults and explain the repair options to homeowners
- Test systems for leaks and repair them before recharging
- Install and replace equipment to code
- Keep clear records of each visit and the work done

What you need
- 4 or more years of experience as an HVAC technician
- Hands-on skill in heat pump service, system diagnostics and leak testing
- Experience with building automation and thermostat installation
- Brazing and soldering on refrigerant lines
- A Refrigerant Compliance Certificate is required
- A Controls Technician Certificate is preferred
