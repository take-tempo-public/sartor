Associate Teacher
Brookside Community Schools — On site, Detroit, MI

Summary
Brookside Community Schools is a nonprofit charter school network committed to giving every child a strong start in reading, learning and community life. The Associate Teacher works alongside a lead teacher to deliver daily instruction, keep classrooms orderly and welcoming, and stay in close contact with families. A current State Teaching Licence is required for this position.

Duties
- Plan daily lessons with the lead teacher and prepare materials ahead of each school day
- Establish and maintain classroom routines that keep students focused and transitions smooth
- Lead small-group instruction and support whole-class lessons as assigned
- Prepare for and take part in parent conferences, sharing student progress and next steps
- Monitor student understanding and adjust activities to meet individual needs
- Keep accurate records of attendance, behavior and student work

Licensure and qualifications
- State Teaching Licence required
- 2 or more years of classroom experience in an elementary setting
- Lesson planning and phonics instruction for early readers
- Classroom management and behavior supports that build positive routines
- Parent communication skills, including regular updates through Seesaw
- Reading Specialist Endorsement preferred
