Associate Teacher
Brookside Community Schools — On site, Detroit, MI

Summary
Brookside Community Schools is a nonprofit charter school network committed to giving every child a strong start in reading and learning. We are seeking an Associate Teacher to work on site in Detroit alongside a lead teacher, supporting instruction and daily classroom life. Candidates must hold a current State Teaching Licence as a condition of employment.

Duties
- Establish and maintain consistent classroom routines that help students start, transition and end the day calmly
- Plan daily lessons with the lead teacher, aligned to network curriculum and student needs
- Prepare for and take part in parent conferences, sharing student progress and next steps with families
- Lead small-group instruction and adapt materials for different learners
- Track student progress through classroom checks and share findings with the instructional team
- Support a safe, positive classroom culture in line with network behavior expectations

Licensure and qualifications
- State Teaching Licence required
- 2 or more years of experience in an elementary classroom
- Demonstrated skill in phonics instruction and lesson planning
- Working knowledge of behavior supports and classroom management
- Clear parent communication, including regular family updates through Seesaw
- Reading Specialist Endorsement preferred
