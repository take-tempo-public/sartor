Case Manager
Harvest Table Family Center — On site, Akron, OH

Summary
Harvest Table Family Center is a family services nonprofit that helps local families build stable, healthy homes.
We are seeking an on-site Case Manager in Akron, OH to carry a caseload of families, conduct home visits and connect households with community resources. A Case Management Certificate is required for this position.

Duties
- Conduct home visits to assess family safety, stability and progress toward goals.
- Complete intake interviews with new families and gather required documentation.
- Perform needs assessments and update them as family circumstances change.
- Write and revise individualized service plans in partnership with each family.
- Coordinate referrals to housing, food and health programs and follow up on outcomes.

Licensure and qualifications
- Case Management Certificate required; Human Services Supervisor Certificate preferred.
- 4 or more years of experience in social services casework.
- Demonstrated skill in referral coordination, benefits enrollment and needs assessments.
- Experience with community outreach and writing clear, timely case notes.
- Working knowledge of Microsoft Access for tracking case data.
