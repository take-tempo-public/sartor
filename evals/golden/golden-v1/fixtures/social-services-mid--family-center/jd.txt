Case Manager
Harvest Table Family Center — On site, Akron, OH

Summary
Harvest Table Family Center helps families in Akron build stable, secure homes. We are seeking a Case Manager to work on site with children and caregivers, connecting them to the services and support they need. Candidates must hold a current Case Management Certificate at the time of hire.

Duties
- Conduct home visits to check on family progress, safety and household needs.
- Complete intake interviews with new families and gather required documents.
- Carry out needs assessments and update them as family circumstances change.
- Write service plans with clear goals and next steps, and review them with each family.
- Keep case notes current and accurate in the agency database.
- Coordinate with schools, clinics and community partners on referrals and follow-up.

Licensure and qualifications
- Required: Case Management Certificate.
- Preferred: Human Services Supervisor Certificate.
- 4 or more years of experience in case management or social services.
- Working knowledge of referral coordination, benefits enrollment and needs assessments.
- Ability to write clear case notes and track client data in Microsoft Access.
- Experience with community outreach to families and local partner agencies.
