Charge Nurse
Lakeview Regional Hospital — On site, Madison, WI

Summary
Lakeview Regional Hospital provides safe, compassionate care to every patient and family in our region. We are seeking a Charge Nurse to lead nursing care on site in Madison, WI, guiding the shift team and supporting patients and families. A current Registered Nurse Licence and an Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card are required.

Duties
- Teach patients and families about diagnoses, medications and self-care before and after discharge
- Chart assessments, interventions and patient education in the medical record accurately and on time
- Supervise nursing aides, set their assignments and check that care is completed
- Lead the shift team and coordinate bed assignments, admissions and discharges
- Work with physicians and other staff when a patient's condition changes

Licensure and qualifications
- Current Registered Nurse Licence and Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card required
- 7 or more years of nursing experience, including time in a lead or charge role
- Sound clinical documentation, patient education and discharge planning skills
- Skill in vital signs monitoring, infection control and triage
- Nurse Manager Certificate preferred
