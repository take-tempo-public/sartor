Charge Nurse
Lakeview Regional Hospital — On site, Madison, WI

Summary
Lakeview Regional Hospital exists to give every patient in our region safe, compassionate care close to home. We are seeking a Charge Nurse to work on site in Madison, WI, lead the unit through each shift and support nursing staff at the bedside. A current Registered Nurse Licence and an Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card are required.

Duties
- Teach patients and families about diagnoses, medications and care after discharge.
- Chart assessments, interventions and outcomes accurately and on time in the patient record.
- Supervise nursing aides, assign tasks and check that care is completed safely.
- Lead the unit as charge nurse, balancing assignments and admissions across the shift.
- Coordinate with physicians and other departments on changes in patient condition.

Licensure and qualifications
- A current Registered Nurse licence and an Advanced Cardiac Life Support Card are required.
- 7 or more years of nursing experience, including time in a lead or charge role.
- Sound clinical documentation and patient education skills.
- Experience in discharge planning, vital signs monitoring and triage.
- Working knowledge of infection control practices.
- A Nurse Manager Certificate is preferred.
