Charge Nurse
Lakeview Regional Hospital — On site, Madison, WI

Summary
Lakeview Regional Hospital provides safe, compassionate care close to home for the communities of Madison and the surrounding region. We are seeking a Charge Nurse to lead the nursing team on site in Madison, WI, coordinating staffing, patient flow and care quality across each shift. A current Registered Nurse Licence is required for this position.

Duties
- Teach patients and families about diagnoses, medications and care at home, and confirm understanding before discharge
- Chart assessments, interventions and outcomes in the health record accurately and on time
- Supervise nursing aides, assign tasks and check that care is completed to standard
- Lead the unit during the shift, balancing assignments and admissions with available staff
- Coordinate with physicians and other departments on changes in patient condition

Licensure and qualifications
- Current Registered Nurse Licence required; Nurse Manager Certificate preferred
- 7 or more years of nursing experience, including time in a leadership or charge role
- Skill in clinical documentation and vital signs monitoring
- Experience with patient education and discharge planning for patients and families
- Working knowledge of infection control practices and triage
