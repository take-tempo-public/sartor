Senior Medical Assistant
Brightwell Urgent Care Group — On site, Raleigh, NC

Position summary
Brightwell Urgent Care Group seeks a Senior Medical Assistant to support providers in a fast-paced, on-site urgent care setting. The successful candidate will perform clinical and administrative tasks, guide fellow medical assistants, and help maintain efficient patient flow. A short list of minimum qualifications appears below, followed by a shorter list of preferred qualifications.

Responsibilities
- Manage clinic supplies and maintain stock levels across exam rooms.
- Coordinate daily clinic schedules to support steady patient flow.
- Take and record vital signs for patients before provider evaluation.
- Schedule follow-up visits and confirm appointment details with patients.
- Guide and support fellow medical assistants on clinical procedures.
- Communicate patient information accurately to providers and front-office staff.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 9 or more years of medical assisting experience.
- Phlebotomy Certificate required.
- Demonstrated skill in specimen handling, blood draws and injections.
- Experience with appointment scheduling and prior authorizations.
- Working knowledge of Epic Systems.
