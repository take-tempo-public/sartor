Senior Medical Assistant
Brightwell Urgent Care Group — On site, Raleigh, NC

Brightwell Urgent Care Group seeks a Senior Medical Assistant to support providers on site in Raleigh, NC. The role oversees patient intake, clinical procedures and front-desk coordination, and guides other medical assistants during busy shifts. Candidates must meet the short list of minimum qualifications below, and a shorter list of preferred qualifications is noted separately.

Responsibilities
- Manage clinic supplies and keep exam rooms stocked for each shift
- Coordinate daily clinic schedules with providers and front-desk staff
- Take and record vital signs for every patient at intake
- Schedule follow-up visits and confirm them with patients before discharge
- Guide and support other medical assistants on clinical procedures

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 9 or more years of experience as a medical assistant
- Certified Medical Assistant Credential required
- Demonstrated skill in specimen handling, blood draws and injections
- Experience with appointment scheduling and prior authorizations
- Working knowledge of Epic Systems
- Preferred: prior experience in an urgent-care setting
