Senior Medical Assistant
Brightwell Urgent Care Group — On site, Raleigh, NC

Position summary
Brightwell Urgent Care Group seeks a Senior Medical Assistant to support providers on site at its Raleigh, NC clinic. The role covers patient intake, clinical procedures and front-desk coordination, and includes guiding fellow medical assistants.
Minimum: medical assisting certificate, blood draws, vital signs and injections.
Preferred: EKG testing and insurance verification.

Responsibilities
- Manage clinic supplies and staff schedules so exam rooms are ready for each shift.
- Take and record vital signs during patient intake.
- Schedule follow-up visits and confirm appointments with patients.
- Perform instrument sterilization in line with clinic protocols.
- Verify insurance coverage before patient visits.
- Guide fellow medical assistants on clinical and front-desk tasks.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 9 or more years of experience in medical assisting.
- Proficiency in specimen handling, blood draws and injections.
- Experience with appointment scheduling and prior authorizations.
- Working knowledge of Epic Systems electronic health records.
- Required certification: Phlebotomy Certificate.
