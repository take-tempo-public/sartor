Lead Teacher
Lakeshore Preparatory Academy — On site, Evanston, IL

Summary
Lakeshore Preparatory Academy is a private nonprofit school committed to giving every child a strong start in learning. We are seeking a Lead Teacher to guide a classroom and support fellow teachers on site in Evanston, IL. A current State Teaching Licence is required for this position.

Duties
- Assess student progress through regular checks and use the results to plan next steps
- Plan daily lessons aligned with school learning goals
- Adapt work for different learners, including small-group support
- Guide fellow teachers in shared planning and classroom practice
- Keep families informed of student progress and classroom goals

Licensure and qualifications
- Instructional Leadership Endorsement required
- 9 or more years of elementary classroom experience
- Experience with lesson planning and reading intervention
- Skill with behavior supports and consistent classroom routines
- Parent communication that keeps families informed and involved
- Comfort using Nearpod and math manipulatives in daily instruction
