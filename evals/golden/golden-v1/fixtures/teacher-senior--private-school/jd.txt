Lead Teacher
Lakeshore Preparatory Academy — On site, Evanston, IL

Summary
Lakeshore Preparatory Academy is a private nonprofit school with one mission: to give every child a strong, caring start in learning. We are seeking a Lead Teacher for an on-site classroom in Evanston, IL. This teacher will guide daily instruction, support fellow teachers and work closely with families. A State Teaching Licence is required for this position.

Duties
- Plan daily lessons that align with grade-level goals and keep a steady pace through the school day
- Assess student progress through regular checks and use the results to adjust instruction
- Adapt assignments and materials so learners at different levels can take part in the same work
- Lead small-group instruction and maintain clear, consistent classroom routines
- Share student progress with families and colleagues, and support fellow teachers with planning

Licensure and qualifications
- State Teaching Licence required
- 9 or more years of elementary teaching experience
- Skill in lesson planning and reading intervention for students at different levels
- Experience using Nearpod and math manipulatives in daily instruction
- Working knowledge of behavior supports and consistent classroom routines
- Clear parent communication, including conferences and regular progress updates
