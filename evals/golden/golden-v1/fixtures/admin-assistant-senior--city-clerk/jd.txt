Senior Administrative Specialist
Ashgrove City Clerk's Office — On site, Ashgrove, OH

Duties
The Ashgrove City Clerk's Office seeks a Senior Administrative Specialist to support the daily operations of the office. The position coordinates schedules for office managers, oversees supply purchasing and invoice handling, maintains public records, and guides administrative staff. The specialist works on site and serves as a point of contact for residents and city departments.

Responsibilities of the position
- Manage calendars and coordinate meeting schedules for office managers.
- Train new administrative staff on office procedures, records handling and front desk duties.
- Order office supplies, compare vendor quotes and track deliveries against purchase orders.
- Review and process vendor invoices for accuracy before approval.
- Prepare correspondence, reports and meeting materials for the Clerk's Office.

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 8 or more years of administrative support experience.
- Working knowledge of invoice processing and purchase ordering.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and calendar management.
- Experience with records filing and travel booking for staff.
- An Office Administration Certificate is required at the time of appointment.
