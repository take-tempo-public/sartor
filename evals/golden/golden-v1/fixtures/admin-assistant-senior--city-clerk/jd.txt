Senior Administrative Specialist
Ashgrove City Clerk's Office — On site, Ashgrove, OH

Duties
The Ashgrove City Clerk's Office is seeking a Senior Administrative Specialist to support daily office operations on site in Ashgrove, OH. The specialist keeps schedules, records, purchasing and correspondence running smoothly for managers and staff, serves as a point of contact for administrative questions, and helps the office deliver accurate, timely service to residents and other city departments.

Responsibilities of the position
- Manage calendars and schedule meetings, appointments and deadlines for office managers.
- Train new administrative staff on office procedures, filing standards and city systems.
- Order office supplies, track deliveries and keep inventory at workable levels.
- Prepare, format and distribute correspondence, reports and meeting materials.
- Coordinate with other city departments and vendors on administrative requests.
- Recommend improvements to office workflows and keep procedure guides current.

Qualifications and conditions of employment
- 8 or more years of experience in administrative or office support work.
- Working knowledge of invoice processing and purchase ordering, including vendor follow-up.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel for tracking budgets, orders and records.
- Experience with calendar management for multiple managers.
- Practical skill in records filing and travel booking.
- Office Administration Certificate required at time of appointment.
