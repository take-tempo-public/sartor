Accountant
Kensington Manufacturing Group — On site, Milwaukee, WI

Position summary
Kensington Manufacturing Group seeks an Accountant to support the general ledger, invoice review, tax schedules and audit preparation for its manufacturing operations. The Accountant will maintain accurate records and work with operations and finance staff to keep reporting timely and complete. Minimum qualifications include experience with budget variance analysis, fixed asset tracking, accounts receivable and journal entries. Preferred qualifications include a professional accounting licence.

Responsibilities
- Review vendor invoices for accuracy, approval and correct coding before payment.
- Prepare and maintain tax schedules supporting periodic filings.
- Assemble audit preparation materials, including reconciliations and supporting documentation.
- Record journal entries and maintain the general ledger.
- Track fixed assets and reconcile the asset register to the ledger.
- Respond to auditor and management requests for financial information.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 5 or more years of accounting experience.
- Working knowledge of budget variance analysis, fixed asset tracking and accounts receivable.
- Experience preparing and posting journal entries.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and SAP.
- Bookkeeping Certificate required.
- Certified Public Accountant Licence preferred.
