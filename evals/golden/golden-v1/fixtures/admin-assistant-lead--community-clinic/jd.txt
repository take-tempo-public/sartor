Office Manager
Sunrise Valley Community Health — On site, Fresno, CA

Summary
Sunrise Valley Community Health provides accessible, patient-centered care to every member of our community. We are seeking an on-site Office Manager in Fresno, CA, to lead daily administrative operations, keep records and budgets in order, and support clinical and administrative staff. An Office Administration Certificate is required for this position.

Duties
- Maintain filing systems and keep paper and electronic records accurate, current and secure.
- Process staff expense reports, checking receipts and approvals before submission.
- Manage office budgets and track spending against approved amounts.
- Select and oversee vendors, including quotes, service agreements and invoices.
- Coordinate supply orders and monitor deliveries to keep the office stocked.

Licensure and qualifications
- Office Administration Certificate required.
- 12 or more years of experience in office administration.
- Proficiency in document formatting and Microsoft Word.
- Working knowledge of Microsoft Outlook for email and calendar management.
- Experience with purchase ordering and invoice processing.
- Experience with travel booking for staff and events.
