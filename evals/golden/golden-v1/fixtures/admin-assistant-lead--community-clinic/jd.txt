Office Manager
Sunrise Valley Community Health — On site, Fresno, CA

Summary
Sunrise Valley Community Health provides accessible, patient-centered care to the communities it serves. We are seeking an Office Manager to oversee daily administrative operations at our site in Fresno, CA. This is an on-site position. Candidates must hold an Office Administration Certificate.

Duties
- Maintain filing systems and keep records current, accurate and secure
- Process staff expense reports and verify receipts against policy
- Manage the office budget and track spending against approved amounts
- Select, coordinate and review vendors for office supplies and services
- Oversee records retention and archiving in line with health center policy

Licensure and qualifications
- Office Administration Certificate required
- 12 or more years of administrative experience
- Strong document formatting skills in Microsoft Word
- Proficiency with Microsoft Outlook for email and calendar management
- Experience with purchase ordering and invoice processing
- Travel booking experience for staff and leadership
