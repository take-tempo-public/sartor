Program Manager
Riverbend Community Partnership — Hybrid, Louisville, KY

Summary
Riverbend Community Partnership connects families with the services they need to stay safe, housed and well. We are seeking a Program Manager to lead our casework team, oversee case records and reporting, and coordinate with schools, clinics and community partners across the Louisville area. This hybrid role answers for program results and staff supervision. A current Human Services Supervisor Certificate is required.

Duties
- Maintain accurate, timely case records for every family in the program and review staff documentation for completeness.
- Prepare clear, well-supported reports for hearings and present case information to courts and partner agencies as needed.
- Coordinate services with schools, clinics and community providers so families receive timely referrals and follow-up.
- Supervise case workers, set caseload priorities and support staff through regular case review.
- Track program outcomes and prepare reports for leadership and funders.

Licensure and qualifications
- Human Services Supervisor Certificate required.
- 14 or more years of experience in social services casework, including team or program leadership.
- Skill in caseload tracking and case notes, with records kept in Microsoft Access or a similar database.
- Experience conducting needs assessments and leading service planning with families.
- Demonstrated client advocacy with schools, clinics, courts and community agencies.
