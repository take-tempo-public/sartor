Program Manager
Riverbend Community Partnership — Hybrid, Louisville, KY

Summary
Riverbend Community Partnership connects local families with the services they need to stay safe, healthy and housed. We are seeking a Program Manager to lead our case management program, guide staff and answer for program results. This is a hybrid role based in Louisville, KY. Candidates must hold a current Mandated Reporter Certificate.

Duties
- Keep case records complete, accurate and current for every client in the program
- Prepare clear, timely reports for hearings that document client needs and progress
- Coordinate with schools and clinics to align services for shared clients and families
- Supervise case managers and review caseloads to keep service quality consistent
- Oversee program budgets and reporting, and present results to agency leadership

Licensure and qualifications
- 14 or more years of experience in social services casework, including team or program leadership
- Skill in caseload tracking and case notes, with working knowledge of Microsoft Access
- Experience conducting needs assessments and leading service planning with clients and families
- A record of client advocacy across schools, clinics and community programs
- Mandated Reporter Certificate required
