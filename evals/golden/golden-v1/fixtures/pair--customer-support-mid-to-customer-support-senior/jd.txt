Senior Support Specialist
Bluebonnet Health Plan — Remote (US)

Bluebonnet Health Plan exists to help every member understand and use their health coverage with confidence. We are seeking a Senior Support Specialist to guide our member support team, coach new agents and resolve complex member concerns. This remote role is open to candidates across the US.

Duties
- Coach new agents on our tools, policies and member communication standards.
- Set support schedules so phone and written channels stay covered during member hours.
- Set team goals for response time and member satisfaction, and review progress with leadership.
- Resolve member complaints, including escalated cases about claims and coverage questions.
- Recommend updates to processes and help content based on recurring issues.

Licensure and qualifications
- 9 or more years of experience in customer or member support.
- De-escalation skills for sensitive conversations about health coverage.
- Phone support experience in a high-volume setting.
- Working knowledge of macros and templates for consistent written responses.
- Experience leading onboarding calls with new members or customers.
- Proficiency with Salesforce and Zendesk.
