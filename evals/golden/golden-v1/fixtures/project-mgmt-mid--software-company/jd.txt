Project Manager
Tandem Software — Hybrid, Boston, MA

About the role
We're Tandem Software, and we're hiring a Project Manager to keep our projects moving from kickoff to wrap-up. You'll work with engineering, product and outside vendors, keep everyone clear on what's happening, and spot trouble before it lands. If you like turning messy plans into finished work, we'd love to talk.

What you'll do
- Run project close-out so deliverables are accepted, records are complete and lessons are written down.
- Take in change requests, assess their impact on scope, schedule and cost, and steer them through approval.
- Keep risk and issue logs current, with clear owners and agreed responses.
- Share regular status updates with the team and stakeholders.
- Work with vendors to keep their deliveries on track.

What we're looking for
- 3+ years of experience managing projects.
- Hands-on use of Asana and Jira for planning and tracking work.
- Strong skills in resource planning and vendor negotiation.
- Clear stakeholder communication and disciplined change control.
- Required: Project Practitioner Certificate.
- Preferred: Agile Delivery Certificate.
