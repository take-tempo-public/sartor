Carpentry Foreman
Brandt Commercial Construction — On site, Denver, CO

Brandt Commercial Construction seeks a Carpentry Foreman to lead carpentry crews on commercial projects in Denver. The Carpentry Foreman directs daily field work, maintains quality and safety standards, and coordinates closely with site management. Minimum requirements include extensive carpentry experience, crew leadership and blueprint reading. A crew supervisor certification is preferred.

Responsibilities
- Build and set forms for concrete footings, walls and slabs
- Read plans and specifications and take accurate field measurements
- Plan daily and weekly carpentry work and assign tasks to crew members
- Prepare material takeoffs and order lumber and supplies for each phase of work
- Inspect completed work for accuracy and quality before handoff to other trades

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 12 or more years of carpentry experience, including framing and stair building
- Proficiency in scaffold erection and power tool use
- Experience with cabinet installation and finish carpentry
- Working knowledge of jobsite safety practices and procedures
- Crew Supervisor Certificate required
- Preferred: experience leading crews on commercial projects
