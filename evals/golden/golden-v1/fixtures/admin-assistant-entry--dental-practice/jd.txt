Office Assistant
Linden Park Dental — On site, Dayton, OH

About us and the job
Linden Park Dental is an owner-run dental practice in Dayton, OH. We are looking for an office assistant to keep the front office running smoothly. This job is on site. You will greet patients, keep schedules in order and help with the paperwork that keeps a small practice going. We are a close team, and we will show you how we do things.

What the job involves
- Manage schedules for the practice managers, including meetings and appointments
- Process staff expense reports and check receipts before they are submitted
- File patient and office paperwork so records are easy to find
- Keep office records up to date and remove outdated files on a regular basis
- Help at the front desk when patients arrive or call

What you need
- At least 1 year of experience in an office or front desk role
- Visitor reception skills and a friendly, calm manner with patients
- Calendar management and travel booking for managers
- Vendor coordination and invoice processing
- Daily working knowledge of Microsoft Outlook
- Office Administration Certificate (required)
