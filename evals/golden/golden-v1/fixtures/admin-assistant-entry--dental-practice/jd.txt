Office Assistant
Linden Park Dental — On site, Dayton, OH

About us and the job
Linden Park Dental is an owner-run dental practice in Dayton, OH. We are looking for an Office Assistant to keep our front office running smoothly. The work is on site, alongside our dentists, hygienists and office manager. You will help managers stay organised, keep records in order and make sure patients and visitors are looked after from the moment they walk in.

What the job involves
- Keep schedules up to date for the practice managers and book their meetings
- Process expense reports and check receipts before they are submitted
- File patient and office paperwork so it can be found quickly
- Keep records accurate and current, both on paper and on the computer
- Help with other office tasks as the day requires

What you need
- 1 or more years of experience in an office support role
- Office Administration Certificate (required)
- Confident use of Microsoft Outlook, including calendar management
- Experience with vendor coordination, invoice processing and travel booking
- Visitor reception skills and a friendly manner with patients and guests
