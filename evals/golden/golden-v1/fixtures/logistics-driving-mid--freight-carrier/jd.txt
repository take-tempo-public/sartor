Class A Truck Driver
Ironwood Freight Systems — On site, Memphis, TN

Position summary
Ironwood Freight Systems, a regional freight carrier, seeks a Class A Truck Driver to transport freight safely and on schedule from our Memphis, TN location. The driver will operate company equipment, maintain accurate records and communicate with dispatch and customers throughout each assignment. At minimum, candidates must hold a valid Commercial Driver Licence and demonstrate safe driving and route planning. Preferred candidates will also hold a Hazardous Materials Endorsement.

Responsibilities
- Keep accurate driver logs and record hours of service in line with federal and company requirements.
- Conduct pre-trip and post-trip inspections and report vehicle defects to maintenance promptly.
- Plan routes that account for traffic, weather, road restrictions and customer receiving hours.
- Secure loads properly and confirm cargo condition before departure and at each stop.
- Coordinate with dispatch on schedule changes, delays and delivery confirmations.
- Complete shipping paperwork and obtain customer signatures at delivery.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- Required: valid Commercial Driver Licence with a clean driving record.
- Required: 5 or more years of commercial driving experience.
- Required: demonstrated defensive driving, route planning and delivery scheduling skills.
- Required: hazmat handling knowledge, routine pre-trip inspections and accurate customer signoff at each delivery.
- Preferred: Hazardous Materials Endorsement.
