Class A Truck Driver
Ironwood Freight Systems — On site, Memphis, TN

Ironwood Freight Systems, a regional freight carrier, seeks a Class A Truck Driver to transport freight safely and on schedule from its Memphis, TN location. The driver will operate tractor-trailers, maintain accurate records and work closely with dispatch. Minimum requirements include a valid commercial driver licence, a Driver Safety Certificate and a clean safety record. Preference will be given to candidates holding a Hazardous Materials Endorsement.

Responsibilities
- Operate Class A tractor-trailers safely on assigned regional routes.
- Keep accurate driver logs and hours-of-service records in line with company and regulatory requirements.
- Inspect vehicles and report defects promptly to maintenance.
- Plan routes with dispatch to meet delivery windows and avoid delays.
- Complete bills of lading and delivery paperwork for each shipment.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 5 or more years of commercial driving experience.
- Driver Safety Certificate required.
- Demonstrated skill in defensive driving, route planning and delivery scheduling.
- Experience with hazmat handling and pre-trip inspections.
- Familiarity with customer signoff procedures at delivery.
- Hazardous Materials Endorsement preferred.
