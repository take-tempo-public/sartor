Mobile Developer
Lumen Reader — Hybrid, Austin, TX

About the role
Lumen Reader is a digital publishing startup building a reading app people actually want to open. We're looking for a mobile developer who likes shipping, cares about readers, and is happy owning features from idea to release. You'll work closely with a small team, have a real say in how the app grows, and see your work in readers' hands quickly.

What you'll do
- Find and fix accessibility issues so every reader can use the app comfortably
- Handle API integration between the app and our content and account services
- Build and improve offline sync so readers can keep reading without a connection
- Work with design and backend teammates to plan, build and ship new features
- Keep the codebase tidy, tested and easy for the next person to pick up

What we're looking for
- 4 or more years of experience building mobile apps
- Strong Swift skills and hands-on experience with App Store release
- Solid Kotlin experience and comfort working with Gradle builds
- Experience automating builds and releases with Fastlane
- Push notifications experience across both mobile platforms
