Project Manager
Elmcrest Builders — On site, Boise, ID

About us and the job
Elmcrest Builders is an owner-run residential builder in Boise, Idaho. We build homes and stand behind our work. We are looking for a Project Manager who wants to be on site, not behind a desk. You will run jobs from permit to handover, work closely with our crews and trade partners, and keep homeowners informed along the way. This is an on-site role.

What the job involves
- Apply for permits and schedule inspections so each phase of the build can move ahead on time
- Run regular meetings with homeowners to review progress, decisions and upcoming work
- Coordinate subcontractors and set the order in which trades come on site
- Walk the site often to check quality, safety and progress
- Keep schedules, budgets and job records current and share updates with the owners of the business

What you need
- 4 or more years of experience managing construction projects, including contract administration and punch list closeout
- Working knowledge of Oracle Primavera for building and updating schedules
- Daily use of Procore and Bluebeam Revu for documents, submittals and drawing markups
- Safety planning experience on active residential job sites
- Construction Safety Manager Certificate (required)
