Senior Brand Designer
Stratford Home Goods — Hybrid, Columbus, OH

Position summary
Stratford Home Goods, a home-goods retailer, is seeking a Senior Brand Designer to shape the visual presentation of its brand across packaging, print and digital channels. This hybrid position is based in Columbus, OH, and reports to the brand team.
Minimum: proven experience in brand identity, layout design and packaging work for retail products.
Preferred: experience with motion graphics.

Responsibilities
- Guide and mentor junior designers, reviewing their work and providing clear, actionable feedback.
- Manage client and stakeholder revisions, keeping each request aligned with brand standards.
- Produce packaging mockups that present product concepts accurately before production approval.
- Maintain consistency of brand identity across packaging, print and digital materials.
- Coordinate with marketing and merchandising teams on campaign timelines and deliverables.

Minimum and preferred qualifications
- 8 or more years of professional experience in brand or graphic design.
- Demonstrated skill in layout design and color theory for retail brand materials.
- Working knowledge of print production and packaging design, including preparing files for vendors.
- Proficiency with Adobe After Effects for short motion pieces.
- Preferred: experience with Canva for building templates used by non-design teams.
