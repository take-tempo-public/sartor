Senior Platform Engineer — Developer Platform

Meridian-scale logistics company, ~400 engineers, ~1,200 services.

About the role
You will own the paved road: the internal developer platform that every product
team ships through. This is a deploy-reliability and developer-experience role,
not a general backend role — success is measured in how fast and how safely other
teams ship, not in features you ship yourself.

What you will do
- Own deployment tooling and the service templates product teams start from.
- Drive down deploy time and change-failure rate across the estate.
- Run and improve incident review; turn findings into platform changes.
- Partner with product teams on migration off legacy VM-based deploys.

Requirements
- Deep production Kubernetes experience at meaningful scale.
- A track record of measurably improving deploy speed or reliability.
- Experience owning developer-facing internal tooling that other teams adopt.
- Comfortable operating a production on-call rotation.

Nice to have
- Streaming data infrastructure experience.
- Prior experience introducing golden-path templates or paved-road tooling.
